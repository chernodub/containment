## Conversion and runtime ownership

Owned scripts live in [tools/assets/kits](../../tools/assets/kits/README.md). Each untrusted archive is downloaded into its own new directory under ignored `tools/assets-src/`. The Python inspector rejects traversal/symlinks and enforces file-count/expansion budgets, records the complete central directory, and extracts data types only. Conversion runs from the repository root, with a fixed selected-model allowlist, local dependency confinement and animation/skin/geometry/image guards. No Blender is used for kit conversion.

The glTF Transform pipeline bakes author transforms, centers the base/footprint, samples original palette colors into body/dark/trim/accent/cloth roles, welds, deduplicates and prunes. Distinct placeholder colors and role extras prevent deduplication from erasing semantic material roles. Quaternius alone uses meshoptimizer simplification with target ratio 25% and relative error bound 1%; achieved per-model counts are recorded rather than claiming that every mesh reached that ratio. Textures/UVs are removed. Output is ordinary GLB with positions/normals and no compressed extension.

`RoomKitAssets` uses the existing `RoomModelCache` and idle prepare-ahead path: three bounded small Kenney keys plus one bounded industrial key. Imports perform no fetch. Production title/settings deferral is asserted before the audio smoke deliberately starts a run. The room owner is included in readiness before material registration and shader warmup. Its 2.5 s deadline prevents late combat additions; cancellation settles immediately, late borrows retire, and callback failure restores the original domestic geometry. Owner/lifecycle tests cover these paths.

Room construction maps roles into ten environment palettes and merges static geometry into at most eight material draws. Shared material, local-light, banded shading, ink/object registration and preparation paths remain responsible for the result. There is no per-frame kit model work, actor/VFX/camera behavior change or navigation mutation. Functional floor vertex colors reuse the conduit pass. The lead-approved density revision supersedes the initial 17k/18k construction limit: admission is capped at 30,000 triangles and the complete root is asserted below 32,000 including floor/connection art. The user budgets remain 330 draws, 3 s worst / 1.5 s typical readiness, zero combat compiles and measured High behavior; those were not relaxed.
