## What worked

- The campaign changes (B): "The teaching grille, first live exclusive and hold grace form a more usable sequence"; "Order now changes a fight after reunion"; the infirmary's 42-to-77 HP forecast "makes a decision tangible" (A).
- The Brute walk (A): "The low hand and rolling shoulder sell a loaded step"; (B) "its weight-loading walk and broad 700-ms wind-up sell a committed swing with a fair recovery."
- The occluded marine (A): the slate silhouette "restore[s] a position I previously had to infer... I would keep it."
- Single dorsal colour per family (A): "groups each animal better". Overload's visible endpoint is now A's reference for a power that ends on its victim.
- Rigs at 7.7 (B): four distinct reasons to move; Glacier is "the strongest single decision in the kit".

## What did not, and where the three agree

1. **Who is hitting me, and how many are there** (A1, B1; fourth round). The isolated strike now reads; the native pack does not. "I know where to dodge before I know which creature to shoot next" (A). Crawlers took 41.7% of Arc 1729's suit and 46.5% of Story Cryo's. A asks for shape, not light: an open gap in the hook, a blunt sac end for the Spitter, two separated blades for the Stalker.
2. **Powers still end on the floor or the caster** (A2, B7, C, gallery DEFECTS). Nova's rim wins the first glance, Slam throws its victim out of frame, Meltdown keeps the eye on the marine, and in dense frames the red hostile wedge outranks the chosen victim; Napalm's live fire is not bounded.
3. **The floor light became a stamp** (C's single change, A3). ROOM36's one bevelled pool per arena is "so uniform it creates the repetition"; no visible source; "the frames look like staged diagrams rather than a dying ship" (C). A: "the fighting floor often stops naming its room". Both ask for the same cure from two sides: a fixture you can see and a pool shaped by it, plus mechanical function in the lanes that survives the landmark leaving frame.
4. **A good move changes the simulation without telling the player** (B2, B3, B5, B6). Seven of nine guardian kills sent the next group back, mostly offscreen; Breacher missed the threshold by 0.17 s. Manual Control's exposed shot saves 1.5 suit on average and all four native visitors left the grille open. The early rehearsal reads as three separate events.
5. **Heavies beyond the Brute** (A4, C): head and back settle as one block; Warden still patch camouflage; Matron a pink cloud; resting Brute masks are over the visor's saturation by +0.28 to +0.59 (gallery measurement against the value rule).
6. **Smaller**: the Safety Interlock edge cue crowds the hotbar and overdrive meter; Armory's mount reads as a black hole in the deck during kiting; the debrief should name the last repair offer and what was taken (B10); the final crossing still hides its standing spot under Pyro's field (A4, B8).

## Lead's reading

The play mean moved because wave 36 spent half its effort on campaign structure, which R31's reading asked for. The art means held or slipped where the work was a uniform treatment applied everywhere (the stamped pool) rather than a designed answer per place.

Wave 37 keeps the split and narrows each item to one finished thing a reviewer can see at 480×270:

- **CONTACT37**: the native Swarm knot, not the staged queue. Three end-on family shapes (hook gap, blunt sac, two blades) under the value rule; the waiting body beside the striker steps clear; the edge shooter's cue stops overlapping the HUD.
- **FX38**: Nova, Slam and Meltdown rebuilt around one in-frame victim with directional impulse into recovery (Overload as the reference); the hostile wedge yields to the chosen victim for the beat only when no attack on the marine is imminent; Napalm as a bounded live patch.
- **ROOM37**: motivate the light. A visible fixture over each pool and a pool shaped by it, different per room (strip, cone, grille shadow); one coloured practical per wall bank; the mount hole in Armory; lane features with a function in four outer lanes.
- **PLAY43**: make earned outcomes visible: the guardian's next group committing at a door the player is looking at and retreating on the kill; Manual Control's firing notch and the escort reacting as the player steps into the lane; the early shooter and hatch in one sightline; the late Spitter crossing a visible gap.
- Then **HEAVY47** (the value-band surfaces on Warden and Matron, head and back lag on the Brute, resting saturation under the visor; the forearm re-rig only if budget allows) and **UI37** (debrief names the last repair offer and what was taken; the Interlock edge cue).

Unchanged from R30 and R31: the remaining questions (does a person see the grille, step out at the console, hear a Crawler under rifle fire) need people playing the game. Both Astra reviewers end on it again.
