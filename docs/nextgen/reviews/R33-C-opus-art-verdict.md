# R33-C — art-director second opinion (Claude Opus, 2026-11-28, gallery final28)

Evidence: 43 images. I looked at 6 screens, 3 sheets, `heavies`, `rigs-in-play` and 2 `creatures37` crowd pairs. From `review480` I took 3 fights, 2 incidents, 8 abilities (3 as sprites), 5 windups (no Crawler), 2 bosses, 1 elite pair, 2 overlaps and 2 conditions. I also opened 3 native frames and 2 motion sheets. I trimmed fights, incidents and windups to stay under the cap.

## 1. Scores

- **First impression: 7.** A strong cel-shaded hero and title type. The backdrop creatures are still grey cut-outs around an empty light hole (`title.png`).
- **Interface: 7.5.** The type discipline, boon vignettes, before/after diagrams and death debrief (`onb14-death-stalker`) are premium. Every screen is still the same teal panel over blur.
- **Combat readability: 6.5.** The red telegraphs and the marine read at 480×270 (`windups--loaded-brute-02`). Stalker and Spitter windups collapse into the marine's own pixels (`windups--loaded-stalker-02`, `-spitter-02`).
- **Environments: 5.5.** Pools now hang from fixtures, but they are hard-edged translucent quads on flat plates (`twenty-arena-calm`, `incident-facing-coolant-plant`). The reactor and the dark condition (`cond15-dark-heavy`) are the high points.
- **Ability effects: 6.** Railbreaker, Napalm and the Pyro overlap (`overlap-pyro-04`) are commercial grade. Whiteout (`whiteout-02`) and Overload (`overload-02`) barely register.
- **Characters and creatures: 6.5.** The Brute walk plants believably (`motion/brute-attack`), and the elite and Warden silhouettes separate by mass (`heavies.png`). The occlusion silhouette works (`ktx2-warden-armory-wall-stun`). Small families are flat tan or lilac paper at native size.
- **Cohesion: 6.5.** Ink, flat fills and the UI agree, but nothing receives light; the art is coloured shapes under a decal.

## 2. Tier

This is a polished early-access indie, a notch below Ruined King / Ascent-class presentation. Its menus and marine outclass its rooms and secondary effects. I would pay $15–18.

## 3. Seven changes, ranked

1. **Turn the pool into light** (`twenty-arena-calm.png`). Replace the quad with a radial falloff from the fixture: hot centre, 40% at the rim, no hard edge. Let it catch the top faces of props. Give characters a two-tone cel response, a lit plane toward the fixture and an ink-dark far side.
2. **Lift the Brutes off the deck** (`creatures37/crowd-relay-racks-native`, final28 half). This build's darker slate Brutes now sit at the floor's value. Raise their top value band by about 15% or add a 1–2 px cool rim on the up-facing planes.
3. **Make the windups break the silhouette** (`windups--loaded-stalker-02`, `-spitter-02`). Raise the Stalker's scythe clear above its body, give the Spitter's sac a hot inner glow, and push both 20% out from the marine before the strike.
4. **Fix the invisible effects** (`whiteout-02`, `overload-02`). Whiteout needs a white frost crust on each body and a pale ground bloom. Overload needs a white-violet core, arc forks to each victim and a short screen-edge flash.
5. **Shade the small families** (`crowds/value14-crowd-crew-checkpoint-01`). Add one shadow band to the crawlers' underside and legs, plus an inner ink line per limb. They read as confetti, not animals.
6. **Break the floors at play scale** (`native-heavy/pyro-pressure-2`). Add cable runs, grime gradients toward the walls, wet patches that catch the pool, and lit wall-top edges for height.
7. **Finish the title backdrop** (`title.png`). Bring the two grey background creatures to the hero's finish and put a bulkhead silhouette into the bright hole.

## 4. Keep

1. The marine's identity kit: the rig-coloured "07" dome pauldron, the cyan chevron visor and white plates, now kept even as an occlusion silhouette (`rigs-in-play.png`, `warden-armory-wall-stun`).
2. The red-only telegraph language, with Railbreaker and the Pyro overlap as the effect benchmarks.
3. The UI system: boon cards with vignettes, route cards and the death debrief diagram.

## 5. Hierarchy, identity, the decision

The hierarchy is nearly complete. The ship is dark, the marine is reliably the brightest value, and lit landmarks (reactor, annulus, relay racks) come third where they exist. The weak link moved: creatures should sit second, but this build's darker Brutes now drift to floor value in teal rooms. Many rooms also have no landmark except the pool itself. The marine keeps one identity from title to play, and the slate occlusion silhouette preserves it. The decision holding the picture back most is still **flat-fill rendering with light only as a floor decal**. Nothing in the world has form light, falloff or rim, so every frame reads as a staged diagram rather than a lit, dying ship.
