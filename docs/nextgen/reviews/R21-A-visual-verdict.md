## 2. What I would pay

**$10 on the visual evidence.** This looks like a small commercial action roguelike with a convincing front end and several saleable combat moments. I would put the Cryo Armory image on a store page. I would hesitate over the next image if it showed Residential Gallery's nearly empty lane or the three waiting Brutes in Swarm Junction. Against the standard I bring to *Hades* for attack posing or *The Ascent* for industrial spaces, the shortfall is clearest between set pieces, when ordinary enemies and a stretch of floor have to carry the picture. [Armory and Residential Gallery](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/twenty-native.png) · [Ordered crowd](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/play25/play25-ordered-crowd-swarm-junction.png)

## 3. Ten defects, ranked

1. **The next danger and a safe response can read differently.** In native Swarm Junction I can see the wide red sector under the marine and a separate filled red circle above it. I cannot confidently order their impacts or pick out the first attacker's limb. Tie the urgent sector to its source with a short bright stroke and give persistent or ranged discs a different edge pattern. [Native Swarm Junction](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/native-heavy/arc-pressure-1.png)
2. **Too many fighting lanes lose the room's identity.** The garden in Communal Atrium and the machinery in Containment Annulus make fine establishing shots. From the marine's position, a grate or generic plate often does the storytelling. Carry a broken irrigation channel through the former and a curved locking track through the latter, flush with the playable floor. [Arena centres](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/twenty-arena-calm.png) · [Kiting](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/twenty-kiting.png)
3. **The waiting crowd exposes the turn order.** Small creatures make a necklace around the marine while three Brutes hold much the same upright pose. The clean gap helps me read the active attack, but the pack looks tame. Keep the attack spacing and give waiting bodies asymmetrical weight, circling steps and different facing angles. [Ordered crowd](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/play25/play25-ordered-crowd-swarm-junction.png)
4. **Melee intent lives mostly on the floor.** A Crawler and a much heavier Brute both project a red fan while their loaded arms blend into their torsos. Watching the enemy should help a player who has lost the edge of the fan. Open a gap between striking limb and chest, then hold the silhouette through the last wind-up frames. [Loaded poses](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/review-sheets/frames-02.png) · [Brute attack strip](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/motion/brute-contact-1.png)
5. **The Breacher loses its defining piece of kit.** The selector sells a broad black strike plate. In the calm crop its broad face is hidden by the shared gun, and in the fight crop the yellow edge can pass for a shoulder. Cant the plate outward and repeat its dark face and yellow lip above the forearm in the common firing poses. [Selector and gameplay crops](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/rigs-in-play.png)
6. **Elites and the Warden depend heavily on rings.** The elite Brute gains a handsome crest, but the Warden in its charge frame could be another large Brute with a floor marker. Give the Warden a unilateral battering shoulder and a planted pre-charge profile that survives occlusion below the waist. [Elite Brute](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/review-sheets/frames-05.png) · [Warden and Matron](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/review-sheets/frames-08.png)
7. **The relay fork asks the player to read arrows to understand cover.** Amber goes straight and cyan bends around the tower, yet the protective lip barely separates from the base. The alternate route is hard to evaluate before committing. Light the top and end caps of the cover and expose the first shelter and both departures in the decision view. [Exposed bank](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/play25/play25-route-0-overload-floor.png) · [Covered bank](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/play25/play25-route-1-overload-floor.png)
8. **Some ultimate attacks lack a distinctive aftermath.** At the sampled Pyro impact the red enemy wedge dominates a broken orange ring much like a mature Napalm pool. Arc leaves a fine polygon web while its victims share a similar crouch. Give Meltdown a directional heat front across bodies and Overclock a stagger that travels through linked targets. [Pyro](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/overdrive/gallery-overdrive-pyro-crew-checkpoint-03.png) · [Arc](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/overdrive/gallery-overdrive-arc-crew-checkpoint-03.png) · [Napalm pair](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/napalm-pair/resting-and-crowded.png)
9. **The marine sometimes shares its brightest patch with the effect.** In the native Overload pressure frame white electricity runs into the white suit. In the Armory's icy field the helmet is another pale shard at thumbnail size. Preserve a narrow dark separation around the helmet and muzzle where friendly effects cross them. [Overload storyboard](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/play/arc/storyboard-overload-floor.png) · [Armory fight](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/review-sheets/frames-12.png)
10. **Mid-fight teaching can cover the thing it teaches.** The “Plasma breaks armour” plaque sits on the central Brute in Arc's Overdrive, exactly where I want to see its reaction. Move the reminder to a stable HUD position after its first introduction, retaining a small armour glyph on the target. [Arc impact](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/overdrive/gallery-overdrive-arc-crew-checkpoint-03.png)

## 4. Five things to preserve

1. **The title's face-off.** The marine aims into the composition, the nearest alien frames the menu from below, and the bright shoulder leads the eye back to the visor. It gives the player a role before the first click. [Title](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/screen-extra/title.png)
2. **The illustrated death lesson.** Crawler fan, Spitter sightline and Stalker lane get different diagrams beside the fatal move and the retry action. It is unusually easy to understand what to try on the next run. [Death debriefs](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/review-sheets/screens-03.png)
3. **The colour and geometry of a single immediate warning.** The red wedge still reads inside Whiteout, and the long Stalker capsule invites a sideways step. Preserve that distinction as combat art gets richer. [Whiteout](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/overdrive/gallery-overdrive-cryo-crew-checkpoint-03.png) · [Stalker wind-up](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/review-sheets/frames-02.png)
4. **Machinery that explains the route through a room.** Relay Racks' conduit, the Armory's circular counter and the broken gate linkage can orient a player without a label. Their silhouettes survive greyscale. [Arena centres](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/twenty-arena-calm.png) · [Greyscale](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/twenty-arena-calm-grey.png)
5. **A power that leaves evidence on victims.** The two Brutes knocked clear of Railbreaker's line sell its force even after the beam has passed them. That spatial relationship is worth more than another layer of sparks. [Railbreaker](../../../artifacts/nextgen/final16/overdrive/gallery-overdrive-breacher-crew-checkpoint-03.png)

## 8. Five investments for one small team and one month

1. **Animate the enemy I need to dodge.** Give Crawler, Stalker and Brute distinct loaded profiles, then align the striking limb with the existing filled sector. Spend the remaining animation time on waiting creatures shifting feet and watching the marine. This improves both threat recognition and the sense of being hunted. Give each one piece of physical evidence that crosses the player's path and points back to the incident. Use the greyscale centre views to judge how much survives without the room tint.
