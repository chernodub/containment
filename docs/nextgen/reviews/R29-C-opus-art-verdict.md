# R29-C — art-director second opinion (Claude Opus, 2026-11-27, gallery final24)

Evidence: 44 images. To stay in budget I used 4 fight frames, 3 incident rooms, 6 abilities and 1 conditions frame. The `creatures33` "now" files are symlinks, so I used the final23 | final24 pairs.

## 1. Scores

- **First impression: 6.5.** The type and the cel marine are confident. The brown-cream Warden reads as cow-hide patches against a muddy purple backdrop (`title.png`).
- **Interface: 7.** A disciplined panel system, and the death debrief is best-in-class. But the shop icons are a different style (glossy dice, photoreal grenades), and every screen is the same teal box over blur (`shop`, `onb14-death-spitter`).
- **Combat readability: 6.** The telegraphs are clean and red only, but at peak pressure the marine is lost among Brute slabs (`twenty-kiting`, `flashfire-03`).
- **Environments: 5.5.** The grading and the ink architecture are solid. The arena centres are still repeating panel decks under soft airbrushed pools, the floor marks read as grey smears, and the props are greybox (`twenty-arena-calm`, `elite-pair-brute` native).
- **Ability effects: 6.** Railbreaker, Meltdown (now a real disc) and Overload land. Cryo Nova is small, pale shards and Flashfire is a few chevrons. Seismic cracks carpet whole rooms (`twenty-native`, Relay Racks).
- **Characters and creatures: 5.5.** The finish is cleaner, but the Brute is an army-green faceted turtle-gorilla that reads as a toy. The small families are dark spiders told apart only by glow. The Crawler's stripes make it a bee, and the Matron's tail is a candy cane (`heavies.png`, `crowds/…swarm-junction-01`).
- **Cohesion: 6.** The creatures and the marine now share flat zones and ink. The glossy shop icons and the soft light washes are the outliers.

## 2. Tier

A competent early-access indie whose menus outclass its in-world art. I'd pay $12–15. No single fight frame yet stands beside Hades or Ruiner.

## 3. Seven changes, ranked

1. **Make the marine win the value fight** (`twenty-kiting`, `flashfire-03`). Lower the Brutes' lit top value by about 15%, add a 2px white-cyan rim to the marine and enlarge the pauldron by 20%, so the marine is the only near-white mass at 480.
2. **Fix the Brute's material** (`heavies.png`). Move to a bruise-slate or oxblood that no floor uses. Cut the back plates from 6–7 stripes to 3 overlapping plates. Add one wet organic accent: a dark open mouth with an inner glow, or eye slits under the brow.
3. **Make elites a rank, not a size** (`elite-pairs/…brute` native). Invert the values (bone plates on a near-black body) and add one crown accent with its own ground-ring shape.
4. **Give the small families shape identity** (`crowds/…swarm-junction-01`). Spitter: a swollen sac. Stalker: scythe forelimbs. Carrier: a tall dorsal pod. Crawler: a low oval with one chevron instead of the bee stripes. Glow on one feature each.
5. **Harden the light** (`incident-facing-breached-loading-bay`, `…infested-workshop`). Replace the blob footprints with hard-edged gobos (slats, beacon wedges, grille shadows), and give each landmark a hot saturated core. Today only the Overload reactor has one.
6. **Author the arena floors** (`twenty-arena-calm`). Replace the smears with hard decals: hazard chevrons, spill, scorch, drag marks. Add one height feature per centre (a lit under-grate trench or a plinth), and take the props past greybox with seams and colour-blocked labels.
7. **Retune the weak effects and cap the persistent ones** (`cryo-nova-03`, `twenty-native`). Cryo needs a cyan core, inked facets and a radius twice as legible. Flashfire needs a full cone with a white-hot core. Seismic cracks should fade in about 1 s and cover no more than 15% of the floor.

## 4. Keep

1. The UI system: rig selector, boon before/after diagrams, route cards and the death diagram.
2. The red-only telegraph grammar, and Meltdown, Railbreaker and Overload as the effect benchmark.
3. Room-by-room grading on the inked hull, and the marine's pauldron, visor and spot pool.

## 5. Value hierarchy, identity, the decision

The hierarchy is three-quarters done. The ship is dark and the marine is lit first in calm frames, creatures sit second by glow, and the dark condition works (`conditions/…dark-heavy`). It breaks at peak pressure, where flat-lit Brutes match the marine's value and outsize him. The landmark is the third tier only in Overload, Annulus and Infested Workshop. The marine keeps one identity from title to play through the pauldron, the visor and the silhouette, though the heroic title proportions shrink to a small, busy figure. The decision holding the picture back most is **soft, unmotivated lighting on generic surfaces**: coloured fog instead of shaped beams. It leaves floors, props and creatures at the same flat mid value, so nothing at play scale feels authored.

## 6. Against R28-C

- **Done:** #1 (flat zones, ink, claws, mask planes), #3 (burning Brutes keep green flanks, `pyro-pressure-3`), #6 (the title creature is crisp).
- **Partly done:** #2 (no longer brass; now a bee; the Breacher's gold wasn't desaturated), #4 (Meltdown fixed, Cryo still weak), #5 (inked, but the windup is still a bigger Brute with no open jaw), #7 (footprints and floor marks exist but are soft; the centres are still bare).
- **Not done:** none outright.
- **Its scores:** broadly fair. I'd shave 0.5 off its interface (the shop icons clash) and off its environments (6 was generous), and I'd hold creatures at 5.5: the finish improved, which exposes the design.
