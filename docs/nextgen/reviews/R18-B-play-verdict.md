## 6. What the commercial leaders still do better

1. *Enter the Gungeon* lets the player keep aiming while reading the next gap. Containment’s clean red footprints approach that standard when both the attacker and exit fit on screen.
2. *Nuclear Throne* makes a single ordinary round changing an enemy’s momentum worth watching. Containment reaches that level of clarity more often with its shotgun than with its pistol or plasma.
3. *Hades* makes a build choice change what the player hopes to encounter next. Containment has several excellent transformative augments, but it spends too many rooms before asking a question specific to the chosen one.
4. A memorable commercial finale gives the last input a purpose specific to the ending. Containment’s sacrifice and final transmission have a clear narrative purpose; its last relay still plays much like an earlier one.
