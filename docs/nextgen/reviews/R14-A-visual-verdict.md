## 2. Calibration

**I would pay US$10 on this visual showing.** This looks like a budget indie with release-quality decision screens and uneven field art. My *Hades* benchmark is whether I can watch the attacker’s shoulders and know when to move. Here I watch the floor. My *The Ascent* benchmark is whether an abandoned workplace tells me who used it. The rabbit wedged against a passenger’s open case does that in one glance at native size. I want to find more things like it. At fighting distance, too much of that story disappears with the case, and too much of the combat survives only as lines and numbers. `crowd-matched/gallery-crowd-stalker-communal-atrium.png`, `rooms/incident-facing-passenger-vault.png`, `twenty-kiting.png`, `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`

## 3. Ten most damaging defects, ranked

1. **I see the next hit’s footprint before I see its owner.** In the Atrium, Stalkers occupy both ends of a bright diagonal lane and a second lane goes dark beneath it. The safe direction is clear. Which animal will be vulnerable afterward is a guess. Widen the striking scythe in top view, open a gap between blade and head on anticipation, and put the brightest bone plane on the loaded side. `crowd-matched/gallery-crowd-stalker-communal-atrium.png`, `crowds/value14-crowd-swarm-junction-swarm-junction-01-grey.png`
2. **The warning edge has unreliable contrast under fire.** The pink lip survives Cryo’s white spray in the staged overlap. In native Pyro, a dark maroon fan meets flames, burn ticks and a dark deck. I can trace it if I stop looking at my target. Keep a narrow light-and-dark border around the committed edge throughout its actionable phase, break the friendly burn perimeter into embers, and combine repeated tick numbers per victim. `overlap/value14-overlap-{cryo,breacher}-communal-atrium-04.png`, `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`
3. **Too many accidents sit outside the fight’s composition.** The Breached Loading Bay incident shows the floor circle below the torn hull. Shielding Gate shows the approach below the gate. That sacrifices two memorable locations in succession through the same framing decision. Move a bent structural member or a displaced door leaf into the approach, so its break and its source share a frame while the dodge lane stays open. `twenty-incident.png`, `twenty-calm.png`
4. **The bodies seldom look as heavy as the actions.** The marine’s knees travel through a large lateral stride under a nearly level weapon. The Brute reaches a strong apex, then spends several cells in nearly the same folded pose. Give the marine a loaded hip and rear-foot push, let recoil compress the firing shoulder, and carry the Brute’s blade past contact before the body settles. These changes would make a hit feel earned and the recovery readable. `motion/marine-run-90.png`, `motion/marine-fire-shotgun.png`, `motion/brute-attack.png`
5. **Large surfaces expose how little separates the ship’s materials.** Freight’s fallen load, crushed base and nearby barrier are broad faces of almost the same brown. Swarm’s prongs, plates and secretion merge into one purple mass. Both scenes have geometry and lack a convincing account of what gave way. Crush one crate corner into a pallet, expose a torn strap under tension, and give the wet growth a different highlight from the buckled metal beneath it. `colliders/incident-cover-freight-hold.png`, `twenty-calm.png`, `twenty-calm-grey.png`
6. **Losing the title marine’s shoulder costs the game a protagonist.** The helmet stripe and dark joints establish armour in play, but the title’s broad yellow pauldron and cyan face become small pale islands around a large gun. All four rig portraits use much the same stance. Carry one broad, saturated shoulder plane and a visible visor edge through overhead lighting, then pose the rigs around their respective weapons. `title.png`, `rig-select{,-arc,-cryo,-pyro}.png`, `rooms/incident-facing-passenger-vault.png`
7. **Elite rank is too subtle to affect target selection.** At 480 wide, the elite Stalker is about as conspicuous as the reticle beside it. The elite Crawler could pass for an ordinary pink-backed bug. Give each a structural distinction that survives silhouette reduction, starting with a forked dorsal shell for the Crawler and one exaggerated hooked limb for the Stalker. `enemy-variants/i11b-calm-{crawler,stalker,spitter,brute,carrier}-elite-crew-checkpoint.png`, `crowd-matched/gallery-crowd-stalker-communal-atrium.png`
8. **Layering still conceals occupied floor.** Cryo’s stacked fields, debris and figures merge at the armory counter, while approaching enemies meet the bottom controls in several kiting views. This costs awareness precisely when looking down to check a cooldown. Reduce frost opacity across actor cores and reserve a visible strip of floor above the controls when near threats approach from below. `builds/v2-full-cryo-armory-armory-04.png`, `twenty-kiting.png` (Crew Checkpoint, Relay Racks, Service Shaft Landing).
9. **The ultimate’s loudest mark can hide its best animation.** Two white 420s and the white Railbreaker beam register a hit instantly, but the flashed victims flatten into paper shapes. Meltdown’s victims burn more convincingly than its smooth pool suggests. Let the beam flash lead into a dark, displaced victim silhouette. Start Meltdown’s heat fracture at the first struck feet and carry it outward. `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-{breacher,pyro}-armory-02.png`, `abilities/v2-meltdown-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-04.png`
10. **The selector spends its best space on the least informative pose.** The front-facing Breacher points the weapon straight at me, which shortens the barrel, hides the chest and makes the rig hard to distinguish from the other three. This is where I am deciding how I want to play. Use a larger three-quarter pose with the rig’s defining equipment clear of the torso. Keep the four diagrams underneath. `rig-select{,-arc,-cryo,-pyro}.png`

## 4. Five things to preserve

1. **The first drill and the death lesson teach the same move.** A red edge around the player, then a small illustration showing how to leave the lane or break sight, makes a retry feel actionable. `onboarding/onb14-drill-during-awakening-bay.png`, `onb14-death-{stalker,spitter}.png`
2. **The menus explain builds in spatial terms.** Ram shoves, Glacier blocks and lightning jumps. The diagrams and boon vignettes let me anticipate those verbs before I equip anything. `rig-select.png`, `augment-draft.png`, `starting-boon.png`
3. **The HUD protects the two numbers I need most.** I can recover 21 health and 05 rounds against a burning floor. Preserve the backing, positions and short keyboard labels. `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`
4. **A few objects make the passengers matter.** An open berth and escaping liquid establish the emergency. A toy beside abandoned luggage makes the later “passengers alive” warning personal. `twenty-calm.png` (Awakening Bay), `rooms/incident-facing-passenger-vault.png`, `rooms/incident-facing-manual-control-chamber.png`
5. **Powers can explain themselves through the victim.** Ice encloses a folded body, fire rises from the shell and a forked discharge joins three separate targets. The player can learn what happened by looking where they aimed. `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-{cryo,pyro,arc}-armory-02.png`

## 8. One month, five investments

1. **Make the attacker readable before the dodge.** Give the character artist and animator the Stalker and Crawler first. Enlarge one striking plane per family, change its outline between anticipation and recovery, and build the elite variation from that same joint. The result should let a player point to the attacker at the end of the red lane. `crowd-matched/gallery-crowd-stalker-communal-atrium.png`, `enemy-variants/i11b-calm-stalker-elite-crew-checkpoint.png`
2. **Finish one crowded Pyro fight and one full Cryo fight as the combat standard.** Give the committed edge stable contrast, pull repeated ticks away from it and keep the victim visible through frost and hit flashes. Apply those priorities to the other two rigs. `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`, `builds/v2-full-cryo-armory-armory-04.png`, `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-breacher-armory-02.png`
3. **Give one artist and animator joint ownership of the marine.** Carry the title’s shoulder and visor into the field, enlarge the selector portrait, and connect hip load, stock recoil and foot plant. It improves the character the player sees in every room and the first choice they make about that character. `title.png`, `rig-select.png`, `motion/{marine-run-90,marine-fire-shotgun}.png`
4. **Finish three pivotal accidents at fighting distance.** In Passenger Vault, separate the rabbit and case by value. In Freight, show where the stack came from and what it crushed. In Manual Control, frame the damaged station and its fatal warning together. These three images can carry a human story across the campaign without filling twenty floors with clutter. `rooms/incident-facing-{passenger-vault,manual-control-chamber}.png`, `colliders/incident-cover-freight-hold.png`
5. **Give each ultimate one memorable aftermath.** Hold a thrown silhouette past the rail flash, leave fractured frost around the actual frozen joints, propagate ignition across the struck shell and let each arc hop finish on a distinct recoil. The four powers already have recognizable footprints. Their victims are where the remaining spectacle will pay off. `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-{breacher,cryo,pyro,arc}-armory-02.png`
