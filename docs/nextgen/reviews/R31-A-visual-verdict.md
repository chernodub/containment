## 2. What I would pay

**US$12.** I would pay for this as a small commercial action roguelike. The death card earns that confidence. It shows the attack, the fatal position and a better move, then puts Retry under my hand. The gap to *Hades* is easiest to see in an ordinary Brute step, when the hand reaches the floor and the chest scarcely seems to load it. The gap to *The Ascent* is easiest to see on the far side of a firefight, where the shape of the machinery should tell me where I am and where I can survive. The menus reach solid commercial indie quality. The recurring bodies and fighting lanes still fall short of the premium claim. This is a judgment from captured play and pose sequences. ## 3. Ten most damaging defects

1. **The next hit has a clear footprint and an ambiguous owner.** At Swarm Junction the filled fan tells me to leave, but the lit claw, rifle and second Crawler occupy the same few pixels. At Interlock I follow a long line down to a partially cropped Spitter beside the hotbar. That delays my next target decision. Keep the fan. Give the imminent limb a short uninterrupted bright root with a dark margin, and move the clipped-source marker just inside the playable viewport. [Ordered pack][ordered] [Edge shooter][edge] [Feint][feint]
2. **Small resting enemies disappear from the count.** In the dark crowd I can pick out violet Carriers and slate Brutes quickly. The straw, lime and ice-blue marks become separate flecks before I can group each one with a body. This makes a waiting pack look safer than it is. Widen one matte dorsal plane per small family and separate its head from its forelimbs in the end-on view. [Dark crowd pair][crowd] [Boot overlap][boots] [Overload pressure][late]
3. **The common Brute looks braced in a still and posed across a stride.** The wedge head and broad palms give a good front view. Side-on, the forearm reaches forward while the chest and back plates travel together. A pack repeats that mechanical gesture. Hold the supporting hand against a deck seam, lower the shoulder as the opposite hand swings, then release heel before knuckles. [Palm plant][palm] [Stride pair][walk] [Heavy lineup][lineup]
4. **The final refuge becomes intelligible late.** I can spot the pale cap in the preview, but my eye follows the incoming shot past it. At the native bank choice the two red pools are much clearer than the floor where I should stand. This weakens a decision that can cost a run. Give the sheltered side a visibly recessed, boot-sized bay and carry the cap’s light around its inside corner. Show the preview shot dying on the outside face. 1. **Give the five brood families distinct shapes at the marine’s feet.** Pair the creature artist with the combat VFX artist. Start at Swarm Junction, identify every resting animal in silhouette, then give only the imminent limb enough separation to point back from its warning. Preserve the quiet palette.
