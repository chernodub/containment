# R27-C — art-director second opinion (Claude Opus, 2026-11-26, gallery final22)

Evidence: 44 images, judged by eye only. The brief's list comes to about 50 images, so to stay in budget I cut 2 fight frames, 1 incident frame, 2 abilities (Flashfire and Overclock; Meltdown was also skipped) and 1 conditions frame. The ability frames are the `-03` frames.

## 1. Scores

- **First impression: 6.5.** The title type is premium. The marine is still a glossy PBR toy with a full visor band and "07", set against cut-out creature silhouettes (`title.png`).
- **Interface: 7.5.** It is one disciplined system with good inked card art and clear pricing (`augment-draft`, `run15-condition-route`, `shop`). The death card's "large" diagram is still a 260×145 thumbnail on a 1920 screen (`onb14-death-spitter`).
- **Combat readability: 6.5.** The lit marine and the red fans and capsules read at 480. The crawlers' and carriers' hot-pink glow now competes directly with the red telegraphs and red projectile rings (`native-heavy/breacher-pressure-3`). Labels like "1260 (3 hits)" still float mid-fight.
- **Environments: 6.** The twenty moods are distinct, and the accident trails give the lanes some direction (`twenty-arena-calm`). The centres are still bare panel slabs. Only the Overload Floor's reactor works as a landmark.
- **Ability effects: 6.5.** Seismic Slam is fixed (dark rubble with amber cracks) and Railbreaker is excellent. The Cryo Nova has a crystal rim but is still a hard-edged stain. Napalm is a thin orange smear, and Pyro's capsule still sprouts a row of stamped flame cones (`pyro-pressure-1`).
- **Characters and creatures: 5.5.** The small families improved: dark bodies, glow marks and floor pools (`creatures31/crowd-relay-racks-native`). The heavies are still saturated mid-green lumps with a flat pale paper wedge for a head (`motion/brute-contact-1`). The Warden reads as a big Brute with a lamp for a face. No jaw is visible (`bosses/…charge-windup`).
- **Cohesion: 6.** Rooms, UI art and the in-play marine share the ink-toon look. The title and selector renders, and the green heavies, sit outside it.

## 2. Tier

A polished early-access indie with AA menus and a bestiary that is half finished. I would pay $15–18, and $25 once the heavies and the title match the marine.

## 3. Seven changes, ranked

1. **Finish the heavies** (`heavies.png`, `bosses/ktx2-warden-crew-checkpoint-charge-windup`). Drop the body to a near-black olive underbody. Keep the lighter dorsal bands for the top plane only, with a thin cool rim light. Replace the flat pale head plane with a modelled skull that has sockets and a split jaw. Make the Warden bone-white and rust, not Brute green. Make the jaw open visibly in the windup and put the glow inside the mouth, not as a lamp on the snout.
2. **Give red back to danger** (`native-heavy/breacher-pressure-3`, `creatures31/crowd-relay-racks-native`). Move the crawler glow to amber-yellow and the carrier glow from magenta to violet. Saturated red and red-pink should be used only for telegraphs and hostile projectiles.
3. **One marine from title to play** (`title.png`, `rig-breacher-equip` vs `rigs-in-play`, `motion/marine-contact-1`). Re-render the title and selector with the in-game toon ramp and ink line, and give the helmet the chevron visor. In play the Breacher "shield" is a grey tray hovering over the shoulder. Mount it on the forearm in front of the torso, tilted about 30°, with a gold rim, so it reads from above as a crescent wall.
4. **Remove aggregate combat text and stamped flames** (`railbreaker-03` "1260 (3 hits)", `overlap-arc-04` "231 (6 hits)", `pyro-pressure-1`). Show per-hit pips at most. Fire should be flicker on bodies and a scorch decal, not upright cones.
5. **A third value tier for incidents** (`incident-facing-coolant-plant`, `-residential-gallery`). Give each incident a coloured practical light (a sparking amber source or a red strobe), a smoke column and a contact shadow, and lift it about 20% above the floor's value. The `overload-floor` reactor is the model.
6. **Break up the arena centres** (`twenty-arena-calm`). Add material zones (grating vs plate), worn walk paths, puddles that catch the marine's light, and foreground cables at the frame edge. Lift the murky purple rooms one stop (`native-heavy/arc-pressure-4`).
7. **Make the death diagram truly large** (`onb14-death-spitter`). Give it half the card's width, with labelled range and cover rings, and redraw the figures with the in-game ink line instead of the tiny PBR sprites.

## 4. Keep

1. The hostile telegraph grammar: red fans, capsules and lanes, consistent for every family and both bosses (`windups/*`).
2. The spot-lit, ink-outlined marine with a rig-coloured dome, readable in every room, condition and crowd.
3. The new species treatment for the small families: a dark carapace, family colour only in the marks, and a soft pool of light on the floor. The Railbreaker beam and the amber-cracked Seismic Slam also stay.

## 5. Value hierarchy, identity, the decision

The hierarchy is almost complete for the small creatures and still incomplete for the heavies and landmarks. The ship is dark, and the marine is lit first everywhere. Small creatures now come second by glow rather than by body colour, which is correct. The heavies, though, are big mid-value green masses that out-weigh the marine in every frame they enter (`arc-pressure-4`, `seismic-slam-03`). Landmarks are lit in one room in twenty. The in-play marine has one identity across all four rigs. The title and selector show a different, glossier figure with a different shield. The decision holding the picture back most is **keeping the heavy line in saturated mid-green with flat, unlit bone planes.** The heavies are the largest shapes on screen, so they set the picture's finish, and they still read as greybox.

## 6. Against R26-C

- **Done:** #3, mostly. The seismic rubble and cracks are good. The cryo has a rim but keeps its hard edge.
- **Partly done:** #2 (a dark carapace for the species, but pink-red glow still on the crawlers and carriers), #1 (the bands show at native size; the Warden has a dorsal sail, but the green is shared and the head is an orb), #5 (only the reactor reads as a landmark) and #6 (accident trails, but the centres are still bare).
- **Not done:** #4 (the title marine) and #7 (the cumulative labels, stamped flames and khaki patches on status-tinted Brutes in `seismic-slam-03`).
- **Its scores:** I agree with them for final21. For final22 I raise ability effects to 6.5 and creatures to 5.5, and keep the rest.
