# R26-C — art-director second opinion (Claude Opus, 2026-11-26, gallery final21)

Evidence: 44 images, judged by eye only. To stay in budget I cut 1 fight frame, 1 incident frame, 2 abilities (Whiteout and Meltdown), the windup crowd and the `crowds` 480 frame; the two `creatures30` crowds cover those. Ability frames are the `-03` frames, assumed to be the impact.

## 1. Scores

- **First impression: 6.5.** The title type and layout are premium. The glossy PBR marine still clashes with the black cut-out creatures behind him and with the toon game (`title.png`).
- **Interface: 7.5.** It is one disciplined system. The inked boon and route illustrations finally match the game's look (`augment-draft.png`, `run15-condition-route.png`), and the supply stop has good icons and pricing. It is generic sci-fi glass, though, and the death card's fix thumbnail is postage-stamp size.
- **Combat readability: 6.5.** The marine's light pool and the red fan, capsule and lane telegraphs stay excellent. But the salmon crawlers and the orange Pyro debris fight the red warnings (`crowd-crew-checkpoint-now-480`, `pyro-pressure-2`), and aggregate labels like "1260 (3 hits)" still float mid-fight.
- **Environments: 6.** The twenty colour moods are distinct and the debris in the lanes helps. The arena centres are still empty mid-grey panel slabs (`twenty-arena-calm.png`). The incident set pieces sit at the floor's value, so they read as clutter rather than landmarks.
- **Ability effects: 6.** The area powers now have mass, and the Railbreaker beam is great. But the Cryo Nova is an opaque grey-teal paint stain with a hard edge that kills the room light (`cryo-nova-03`, `overlap…cryo-04`), and the Seismic Slam is a brown hex rug (`seismic-slam-03`).
- **Characters and creatures: 5.** The marine is a 7. The heavies are stacked green cubes: the "plates" read as boulders, the hide pattern is invisible at 480, and the Warden is a bigger Brute with an orange mouth (`heavies.png`, `bosses…charge-windup`). The small families are toy-coloured. The poses now have anticipation (`motion/brute-attack.png`).
- **Cohesion: 6.** UI art, rooms and marine now share the ink-toon language. The title and selector renders and the candy creature palette sit outside it.

## 2. Tier

A polished early-access indie with AA menus and prototype-grade creatures. I would pay $15, or $20–25 once the bestiary matches the marine.

## 3. Seven changes, ranked

1. **Redesign the heavy silhouettes** (`heavies.png`, `elite-pairs/…brute`). Replace the cube stack with 3–4 large curved, overlapping dorsal plates, a spine ridge and an asymmetric claw arm. Split the values: near-black underbody, lighter dorsal plates. Give the Warden its own read: an elongated crest, bone-white plates, 1.5× height and no shared green.
2. **One species palette** (`creatures30/ordered-crowd-now-480`). Give every family a shared bruise-dark carapace and confine each family's hue to its glow marks. Take the salmon off the crawler body so red belongs only to telegraphs.
3. **Finish the area surfaces** (`cryo-nova-03`, `seismic-slam-03`). Cryo: fade to 25% opacity at the centre, put a bright frost-crystal band on the rim and add specular glints. Seismic: dark rubble with thin emissive orange crack lines and a few raised plates that cast shadows, not a flat brown texture.
4. **Match the title and selector marine to the game** (`title.png` vs `rigs-in-play.png`). Render them with the in-game toon ramp and ink line, or paint key art. Use the chevron visor, not the full blue band. Redraw the title creatures in the same medium as the marine.
5. **Stage the incidents as the third value tier** (`incident-facing-armory`, `-coolant-plant`, `-transmission-chamber`). Give each one a coloured practical light (a sparking orange source, a failing red strobe), a steam or smoke column, a contact shadow, and a value 20% above the floor.
6. **Break up the arena floors** (`twenty-arena-calm.png`). Add material zones: worn walk paths, grating vs plate, hazard paint, coolant puddles that pick up the marine's light, and scorch marks near incidents. Add foreground pipes or cables at the frame edges for depth.
7. **Remove leftover combat text and stamped flames** (`flashfire-03` "144 (8 hits)", `pyro-pressure-2`). Drop the cumulative labels. Attach burn flicker to bodies instead of upright floor sprites. Mute the khaki "frozen camo" patches on status-tinted Brutes (`seismic-slam-03`).

## 4. Keep

1. The hostile telegraph grammar: red fans, capsules and lanes, consistent across every family and both bosses.
2. The spot-lit, ink-outlined marine with a rig-coloured dome, readable at 480 in every room and condition (`conditions…dark-active`).
3. The UI system, now with inked card illustrations: boon before/after diagrams, route cards and the cause-and-fix death debrief.

## 5. Value hierarchy, identity, the decision

The hierarchy is three-quarters complete. The ship is dark, and the marine is lit first everywhere, including the dark condition. Creatures come second by hue and outline more than by value: the Brutes sit near the floor's value. Landmarks are the missing tier. Outside the reactor and annulus rooms, the incident set pieces are grey debris at floor value, and in fights they fall out of frame. In play the marine has one identity across all four rigs. The title and selector still show a glossy, bulkier PBR toy with a full visor band and "07". The decision holding the picture back most is **building creatures from blocky primitive facets in saturated toy colours.** Next to a well-finished marine, the aliens read as greybox gorillas and candy bugs rather than a threat.

## 6. Against R25-C

- **Done:** #3 (the floor stencils are demoted and out of the fight area). #2 is largely done: the area powers have fill mass, but it is flat.
- **Partly done:** #1 (there are plates and a rim, but they read as cubes, and the Warden is still a big Brute), #5 (the anticipation lean is visible in the strip), #6 (debris was added to the lanes, but the centres are still empty) and #7 (fewer numerals, but cumulative labels and stamped flames remain).
- **Not done:** #4 (one species palette).
- **Its scores:** I agree with them for final20. For final21 I raise ability effects to 6 and keep everything else the same. The improvements are real, but they are incremental.
