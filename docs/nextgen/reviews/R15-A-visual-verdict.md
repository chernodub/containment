## 2. Calibration

**I would pay US$10.** This looks like a budget commercial indie with a release-quality interface and uneven combat art. My *Hades* comparison is the instant before a hit. In Awakening Bay I can watch the Brute lift its weapon and step out. In the shaft fight I watch a red strip, then look for the creature that made it. My *The Ascent* comparison is whether a workplace stays recognizable while I shoot across it. The wet tracks out of the open berth pass that test for an entire encounter. A grille in a violet pool cannot carry Shielding Gate the same way. Those are the gaps between this build and a premium action game. `onboarding/onb14-drill-during-awakening-bay.png`, `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`, `play/arc/storyboard-awakening-bay.png`, `twenty-incident.png`

## 3. Ten most damaging defects, ranked

1. **The danger area reads faster than its attacker.** In the Arc overlap I can follow the left pink curve around the marine. At its point, a white chain flash, a 45 and the small body meet over a horizontal grille. In the native shaft fight two strips converge under the marine. I know to leave, but choosing which enemy to punish afterward becomes guesswork. Open a gap between the loaded limb and torso, hold that pose through commitment, and move numbers away from the attack origin. `overlap/value14-overlap-arc-communal-atrium-01.png`, `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`
2. **The lower HUD hides approaching bodies.** In Relay Racks the incoming group runs into the weapon tray. On 720 Low in Overload Floor, health bars and arrows survive where feet and parts of torsos vanish. The player loses the pose that distinguishes approaching from striking. Widen the unobstructed strip above the tray with a small camera lead toward the nearest lower threat, and collapse inactive weapon descriptions during combat. `twenty-kiting.png` (Relay Racks, Crew Checkpoint), `review480/720--gallery-low-kiting-overload-floor-01.png`
3. **Several incidents fall outside the playable composition.** Shielding Gate gives almost its entire incident frame to the approach. In Breached Loading Bay the empty circular floor mark gets more space than the torn hull. That costs the player a landmark for navigating the fight. Bring a bent gate leaf and a strip of peeled hull plating down into their respective approach lanes, with their attached ends still visible. Preserve the clear dodge route. `twenty-{incident,kiting}.png` (Shielding Gate, Breached Loading Bay)
4. **The marine loses a face between the title and play.** I can pick the pale gunner out of a crowd. I recognize the title’s person much less readily once cheek, visor rim, firing arm and weapon all catch the same light. At native size the Arc muzzle flash almost completes that merger. Keep one dark visor aperture, one broad shoulder cap and a dark break behind the gun in every rig palette. Light the selector with that same arrangement. `screen-extra/{title,rig-arc-equip}.png`, `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-{arc,breacher}-armory-03.png`
5. **Elite rank has too little silhouette.** Side by side, both Carriers have much the same high pouch and hanging legs. The Stalker pair asks me to notice a health bar before I notice heavier anatomy. That is a poor basis for target priority in a swarm. Split the elite Carrier’s sac into two unequal lobes and extend one hooked forelimb on the elite Stalker. Give each remaining elite one comparably large anatomical distinction. `elite-pairs/gallery-elite-pair-{carrier,stalker,crawler,brute,spitter}-crew-checkpoint.png`
6. **The bodies do too little of the acting.** The marine’s stride opens and closes under a steady chest. The isolated Crawler visibly rolls under rifle fire, but in the Stalker pack the surviving hit, broken stride and death take close study even with captions. Put the marine’s weight over the planted foot on braking. Give the small survivor a lateral buckle and the lethal hit a lower, asymmetric resting silhouette. `motion/quarter/{marine-stop-start,crawler-weapon-sequence-rifle}.png`, `motion/scenes/pack-stalker-contacts.png`
7. **The ship conditions vary in how well they explain themselves.** Without the label I can infer electricity from the branching lanes and suction from debris heading into a grate. Coolant resembles overlapping translucent zones, brood resembles a pod encounter, and the sealed door could be an ordinary closed exit. Emergency darkness costs actual information: at upper right the organs remain visible after the animals’ legs disappear. Give the leak a visible source, run heat from the pods into deck seams, put locking bars across the shutter and rake a narrow emergency light across approaching bodies. `conditions/cond15-{conduit,vent,coolant,brood,sealed,dark}-active-atrium-communal-atrium.png`
8. **The biggest powers need a stronger physical consequence.** Railbreaker’s four 420s explain its power better than the briefly bleached victims. Meltdown puts similar upright tongues beside different bodies, and Arc’s duplicate marine is easier to see than the enemy’s response. Hold a dark displaced silhouette after the rail flash, run ignition along each struck shell, and give one slowed victim a leg suspended midway through a step. `abilities/v2-railbreaker-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-02.png`, `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-{breacher,pyro,arc}-armory-03.png`
9. **Some accidents look arranged on a clean deck.** Freight’s tilted grey block and nearby barrier have broad unbroken faces. At Swarm Junction the struts and growth share a purple middle value. I see a pile in each room before I understand what failed. Expose one sheared pallet under the Freight load and drag its strap across a seam. Give the Junction’s wet secretion a continuous highlight that crosses the dry broken metal. `twenty-{calm,calm-grey,incident}.png` (Freight Hold, Swarm Junction)
10. **The two rig-start actions can point to different characters.** With Arc inspected, “Equip Arc” sits beside “Quick start · Cryo.” In the quick-start state, the bright primary action selects the rig I am *not* looking at. That can undo a decision made with the four diagrams directly below. Keep the inspected rig as the stable primary action and label the secondary action “Repeat last rig · Cryo.” `screen-extra/rig-arc-{equip,quick}.png`

## 4. Five things to preserve

1. **The first room teaches through its geography.** I can follow the leak back to the open berth, track the approaching Brute and see the sidestep opportunity in the same space. Keep the glancing light, tracks and quiet floor beside the spill. `play/arc/storyboard-awakening-bay.png` (entry through 11.4 s), `onboarding/onb14-drill-during-awakening-bay.png`
2. **The build diagrams answer a player's question.** The Arc cards show a dash ending in a fork, a chain visiting targets in order and a placed pylon. The boon art then shows what a modified attack will do to a body. Keep the trajectory, target and outcome together. `screen-extra/{rig-arc-equip,starting-boon,augment-draft}.png`
3. **The warning and death lesson share a vocabulary.** The combat wedge gives a direction to escape, the Stalker debrief illustrates moving across a lane, and the Spitter card puts cover between shooter and marine. A retry starts with a specific move to try. `overlap/value14-overlap-breacher-communal-atrium-01.png`, `screen-extra/onb14-death-{stalker,spitter}.png`
4. **Health and ammunition remain readable under pressure.** In the native Pyro frame I can retrieve 60 and 00 against orange cracks, damage ticks and crossing red lanes. Keep their local dark backing and fixed positions. `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`
5. **The best powers tell different spatial stories.** Bulwark seals a line between two posts, Tesla connects marine, pylon and target, and Cryo breaks the floor around frozen legs. These differences survive changes of colour and make four rigs worth inspecting. `abilities/v2-{bulwark,tesla-pylon}-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-02.png`, `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-cryo-armory-03.png`

## 8. One month, five investments

1. **Put the next hit into the attacker’s body.** Pair a creature artist and animator on Stalker, Crawler and Brute. Give each a loaded side visible from above, a distinct strike silhouette and an exposed recovery. Use the same joints to differentiate elites. The practical target is being able to point to the dangerous animal while still watching the marine. `overlap/value14-overlap-arc-communal-atrium-01.png`, `elite-pairs/gallery-elite-pair-stalker-crew-checkpoint.png`, `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`
2. **Finish the marine across all three viewing distances.** Put the title, selector and a field-sized figure on one artist’s screen. Carry the dark visor and shoulder block through the four rig colours, then have the animator connect braking and recoil to the planted leg. This improves the character visible in every encounter. `screen-extra/{title,rig-arc-equip,rig-breacher-equip}.png`, `motion/quarter/marine-stop-start.png`, `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-arc-armory-03.png`
3. **Recover the last few metres of combat visibility.** Give a rendering/UI engineer the shaft Pyro crossing, the stacked Cryo build and the lower approach in Relay Racks. Move repeated ticks away from strike origins, thin friendly layers over occupied floor, keep threats clear of the tray and light enough of a blackout attacker to show its facing. `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`, `builds/v2-full-cryo-armory-armory-04.png`, `twenty-kiting.png`, `conditions/cond15-dark-active-atrium-communal-atrium.png`
4. **Recompose three encounters around what happened to the ship.** Put a displaced gate leaf in the Shielding Gate approach, carry torn hull into the Loading Bay’s circle and pull the abandoned operator position into Manual Control’s fight. Use the berth spill as the standard: a player can understand the source, the path and the consequence while moving. `twenty-{incident,kiting,heavy}.png`, `play/arc/storyboard-awakening-bay.png`
5. **Spend the VFX and animation pass on the struck bodies.** Give Railbreaker a readable dark tumble after the flash, spread Pyro ignition across the shell, arrest a limb in Arc and keep an articulated victim visible inside Cryo. The four powers already have different footprints. What happens to the thing I aimed at is what would make the shot worth repeating. `abilities/v2-railbreaker-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-02.png`, `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-{breacher,pyro,arc,cryo}-armory-03.png`
