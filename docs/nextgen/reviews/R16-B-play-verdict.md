## 2. Calibration

**Containment has a commercial-indie combat system. ## 6. What the commercial leaders still do better

1. **Keep the next step visible.** In *Enter the Gungeon*, the attraction of a dense volley is finding a route through it. Here, an accurate red shape can point toward a landing occupied by UI or friendly frost.
2. **Make a good response pay off on the body.** *Nuclear Throne* is my benchmark for identifying what an ordinary shot did while continuing to move. Containment’s shotgun approaches that standard more often than its rifle.
3. **Teach through recoverable failure.** A first missed dodge should earn another attempt at the same readable situation. The bonus Brute can instead send a player back to loadout.
4. **Give repeated encounters a changing relationship to the build.** A *Hades* run can give a familiar room new stakes through the reward I chose to pursue. Ice Road changes my next fight that much. Three successive salvage decisions seldom do.
5. **Give a boss opening room to breathe.** Landing the Warden stun or stopping an entire brood deserves one unmistakable beat in which I can see what I achieved and choose how to spend it.

## 8. One more month, five investments

1. **Rebuild the first clear around safe practice.** Bank Awakening Bay, offer the drill, show the footprint crossing, then highlight one recovery shot. A player who misses twice should still reach their first draft knowing what to try next.
2. **Make the attacker, marine and escape route readable together.** Start with the actual Diagnostic Gallery pressure frame and Pyro finale. Give the committed enemy priority over the dock, friendly residue and incidental numbers, then carry the rule through every rig.
3. **Teach two combat decisions in the midgame.** Use the first plated heavy for a plasma lesson and the first relay receiver for a block-then-cross lesson. Pay each off visibly on the enemy or cover. Keep the later, dangerous versions intact.
4. **Finish the ordinary hit and the earned opening.** Give rifle contact a directional reaction, the Warden a readable slump, and brood interruption a visible collapse through the pods. Audition their cues together with sustained fire and a real Overdrive.
5. **Give one early junction ownership of a later fight.** Offer a plate-breaking route with plasma supply against a brood route with rockets. Replace one repeat guardian appointment, name the chosen destination, and show the result in the debrief. A second run should give the player a decision worth reversing.
