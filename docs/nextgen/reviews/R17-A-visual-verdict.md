## 2. Calibration

**I would pay US$10 on the visual evidence.** The interface and the first junction look ready to sell. The twenty-room campaign looks like a budget commercial indie. My *Hades* benchmark is being able to choose the enemy to interrupt while stepping out of its attack. Here I can do that in a duel and lose it under Pyro pressure. My *The Ascent* benchmark is recognizing a workplace from the ground I am fighting across. The thawed berth passes that test. Shielding Gate’s violet grille could belong to half the ship. The premium claim turns on those repeated seconds. These judgments cover poses, framing and visible consequences. Stills leave timing, input feel and performance unscored. ## 3. Ten most damaging defects, ranked

1. **Fire hides the enemy I need to interrupt.** Diagnostic Gallery puts dark bodies, orange fissures and repeated fire patches inside one bright mass. I can read several damage values before I can find their owners. Reduce the brightness and density of lingering fire around a winding-up enemy, then carry one bright ignition along the shell that actually took the hit. [Native Pyro peak][diag] · [Pyro overlap][f5]
2. **Small predators lose their attacking end.** The regular Stalker’s violet back survives reduction after its forelegs disappear. Two Stalkers in red lanes near the marine are identifiable largely from their outlines. Give the leading plate one broad lit bevel and open a gap between the loaded claw and the head in the lunge pose. [Regular and elite][f3] · [Manual Control pressure][py]
3. **The larger Brood lack a convincing face at fighting distance.** The Brute’s slate blade has a stronger contour than its jaw. The Matron’s pale joints and swollen abdomen explain her role, but leave her facing ambiguous. Establish a dark neck break, one forward-facing jaw plane and a deliberate overlap where the shoulder plates bear weight. Check both animals from the gameplay camera. [Brute poses][brute] · [Matron beside Carrier][matron]
4. **Several rooms lose their identifying feature while the player circles.** Shielding Gate leaves its torn leaf at the top and lights a grille near the centre. Breached Loading Bay gives the scratches more screen space than their source. Extend a broken gate guide and a strip of peeled hull along the approaches, keeping the movement lanes clear. [Incident views][incident] · [Staged kiting][f1]
5. **Some ultimates separate impact from consequence too quickly.** Railbreaker’s two airborne bodies are already well above their 420s and the beam in frame 03. Arc needs “Link ends here” to identify a fairly ordinary-looking last victim. Give the Rail landing a directional skid under each body and have the final Arc target lock one limb in a bright forked discharge. [Rail aftermath][rail] · [Arc endpoint][arc]
6. **The marine’s upper body absorbs too little apparent force.** The long split stride says movement, but the brake samples change the weapon line very little. Rifle and shotgun are easier to distinguish by barrel than by kick. Compress the leading knee on braking, rotate the shooting shoulder around the planted hip, and hold the heavy weapon’s recovery pose long enough to read. [Marine stop, rifle and shotgun][marine]
7. **The bottom approach still loses actors.** In the Armory Cryo fight, health bars and heads collect just above the dock while legs and other bodies go beyond the viewport. A narrower dock helps with text but cannot reveal a body outside the shot. Use this particular lower approach to tune threat-aware framing within the existing hero clearance, then tuck inactive dock detail inside its current silhouette. [Armory rig fight][f2] · [Gate kiting][f1]
8. **Deck damage often stops before it explains the accident.** Freight’s tilted load has a blank broad face and a few fragments. The restraint and the point that gave way are hard to infer even in the quiet view. Run a snapped restraint from a marked floor socket to one bent corner of the load and carry a single scrape back along its path. [Freight entry][calm] · [Freight centre][arena]
9. **Ship conditions sometimes resemble interface graphics laid on the deck.** Coolant makes overlapping translucent discs, conduits make luminous ladder shapes, and the sealed exit’s mechanism is difficult to read beside the marine. Give the first pool a feeding leak, run the energized lanes from a visible junction box, and show a locking bar across the shutter. [Coolant and venting][f7] · [Conduits][conduit] · [Sealed exit][sealed]
10. **Later route art undersells the choice.** The Plate-breaker and Brood cards show two different confrontations. The later “Stay on mission” card pictures a nearly empty floor circle beside a requisition card with a tiny cost. Put a recognizable threat in the standard-route vignette and set the alternate price alongside its title. [First junction and later requisition][s5]

## 4. Five things to preserve

1. **The berth explains what happened without a caption.** A missing front, footprints and reflected light join the source of the leak to the player’s path. [Awakening Bay storyboard][opening]
2. **The first route choice has a payoff.** The cards show an armoured target and a brood encounter, and the destination explicitly recalls the choice. [Route cards][s5] · [Arrival banners][f2]
3. **The death lessons give actionable spatial advice.** The three illustrations distinguish backing away, breaking sight behind cover and stepping sideways. [Death debriefs][s3]
4. **The four rigs have distinct geometry.** A straight rail, an ice barrier, a widening flame front and a chain between bodies each suggest a different positioning decision. [Rig diagrams][s1] · [Full builds][f4] · [Armory rig fights][f2]
5. **The most urgent information survives darkness.** In the native Gate peak I can find the white suit, 54 health, seven rounds and the hostile lunge below my feet in one glance. Preserve that local contrast. [Gate pressure][gatefight]

## 8. Five investments for one month, in order

1. **Make the next attacker readable through a full Pyro or Arc build.** Pair a combat VFX artist with the creature animator. Reserve contrast at the firing jaw or loaded claw, let friendly residue fade around that silhouette, and carry the projectile’s direction back to its owner. Review Diagnostic Gallery and Interlock side by side. [Pyro peak][diag] · [Arc peak][inter]
2. **Finish the Brood’s heads, striking limbs and load-bearing joints.** Start with the Stalker, Brute and Matron. A clear facing, one exposed attacking joint and a visibly compressed supporting leg would improve both threat recognition and perceived craft in nearly every encounter. [Family pairs][f3] · [Brute][brute] · [Matron][matron]
3. **Carry four room incidents into the actual fighting lanes.** Give Gate a broken guide, Bay a peeled hull seam, Freight a snapped restraint and Swarm a change from dry rib to wet secretion. The berth already shows how one continuous physical trace can tell the story. [Incidents][incident] · [Centres][arena] · [Berth][opening]
4. **Give each ultimate a distinct victim pose and recovery.** Show heat travelling across a Pyro target, a last joint locking under Arc, a directional landing after Railbreaker and one interrupted step held in ice. Spend the brightest frame on the struck body. [Full builds][f4] · [Overdrives][f5] · [Rail aftermath][rail]
5. **Put weight through the marine’s stock, shoulder and planted foot.** Keep the new silhouette. Pose a strong brake and distinguish the rifle’s short impulse from the shotgun’s heavy return at gameplay scale. Restore a dark lower edge to the visor so head direction survives a bright pool of light. [Marine poses][marine] · [Native field suit][py]
