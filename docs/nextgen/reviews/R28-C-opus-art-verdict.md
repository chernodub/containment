# R28-C — art-director second opinion (Claude Opus, 2026-11-26, gallery final23)

Evidence: 43 images, judged by eye. To stay in budget I used the `twenty-incident.png` sheet instead of four incident frames, used 5 fight frames instead of 6, 1 overlap frame instead of 2, 1 conditions frame instead of 2, and skipped the separate windup crowd frame.

## 1. Scores

- **First impression: 6.5.** The title type and the redrawn marine are premium. The creature in the centre is an unresolved brown-green smear with white blotches (`title.png`).
- **Interface: 7.5.** It is one disciplined system. The boon cards have before/after diagrams, the shop icons are clean, and the death card now has a properly sized inked diagram (`augment-draft`, `shop`, `onb14-death-spitter`).
- **Combat readability: 6.5.** Red now means danger only, and the telegraphs read at 480. But amber crawlers in their lunge pose look like brass cartridges, and they share a hue with the Breacher's gold, the pickups and the projectiles (`crowds/…relay-racks-01`, `overlap-pyro-now-480`).
- **Environments: 6.** The per-room grades are strong, and Relay Racks is genuinely good at native size. The arena centres are still big, uniform striped slabs. Incident lights barely register except at the Overload reactor and the Annulus ring (`twenty-arena-calm`, `twenty-incident`).
- **Ability effects: 6.** Railbreaker, Seismic Slam and Flashfire are good. Cryo Nova is a grey dust smear, Whiteout is pale-blue blobs, and Meltdown is a small orange scribble rather than an event. No effect throws light onto the floor (`fx33/*-peak-now-480`).
- **Characters and creatures: 5.5.** The marine is good. The creatures have noisy brush-mottle camouflage on soft tube topology, sausage fingers and flat card masks. The Warden is a brown mound with a white sticker rim (`heavies.png`, `bosses/…crew-checkpoint-charge-windup`, `motion/brute-attack`).
- **Cohesion: 6.5.** UI, title, selector, rooms and marine now share one ink language. The creatures' painterly mottle is a second style.

## 2. Tier

A polished early-access indie with AA menus and a bestiary that is still the weak link. I would pay $15, and $25 when the creatures are finished to the marine's standard.

## 3. Seven changes, ranked

1. **Re-skin the creatures in the marine's cel language** (`heavies.png`, `motion/brute-attack`). Replace the brush-noise mottle with 2–3 flat value bands, ink interior lines and one rim light. Taper the fingers into 3 distinct claws. Give the bone masks a second plane (brow and cheek) so they aren't paper cards.
2. **Stop crawlers reading as ammunition** (`crowds/value14-crowd-relay-racks-relay-racks-01`). Keep the dark body silhouette with legs splayed, and limit amber to 2–3 dorsal spines and the eyes, not a full-body glow. Shift the Breacher's and the pickups' gold toward a desaturated ochre so the amber belongs to the crawler.
3. **Status effects must not erase species** (`overlap-pyro-now-480`: burning Brutes become tan teddy bears; `whiteout-peak`: frozen creatures become pale-blue blobs). Apply status as a rim plus a top-plane tint of 40% at most, and keep the family marks and the dark underbody visible.
4. **Make the cold and heat ultimates events** (`cryo-nova`, `whiteout`, `meltdown` peaks). Cryo should be hard-edged crystal shards with white cores and a cyan floor-light pulse, not a fog. Meltdown should be a filled heat disc at its true radius, with a bright edge ring, an orange light on every nearby surface and a dark scorch afterwards.
5. **Give the Warden a boss silhouette** (`bosses/ktx2-warden-crew-checkpoint-charge-windup`). Drop the white outline and use the shared black ink. The jaw must break the body outline in the windup: open the lower jaw down and forward, with the glow deep in the mouth. Make the sail vertical and taller so the top-down shape is unique and isn't a bigger Brute.
6. **Redraw the title creature** (`title.png`). The centre of the key art is mud. Give it the same crisp ink, flat bands and a lit rim as the marine opposite it. It is the first thing a buyer sees.
7. **Make landmarks the third value tier and break up the floors** (`twenty-incident`, `twenty-arena-calm`). Double the intensity and radius of each incident's practical light, add smoke or sparks catching it, and place 2–3 mid-scale floor clusters per arena (cable runs, debris, spill decals) in the light pool.

## 4. Keep

1. The UI system: rig selector, boon cards with rule diagrams, route cards and the death diagram.
2. The ink-outlined architecture with distinct grading per room. Relay Racks and Containment Annulus are the benchmark.
3. The marine's identity (rig-coloured pauldron, chevron visor, spot pool) and the red-only telegraph grammar.

## 5. Value hierarchy, identity, the decision

The hierarchy is mostly there. The ship is dark and the marine is lit first in every frame. Creatures come second by glow, but the near-black Brutes now sink into the purple and teal floors (`ordered-crowd-native`, Swarm Junction). There a cool rim light is needed, not more body value. The amber crawler swarms sometimes out-shine the marine. The landmark is the third tier in only about three rooms of twenty. The marine now keeps one identity from title through selector to play, a real gain. In play the Breacher's shield still rides over the shoulder like a tray (`motion/marine-run-45`). The single decision holding the picture back is **the creature surface treatment**: painterly noise on soft blobs, next to a crisp cel marine and crisp ink architecture. Two rendering styles share every fight frame, and the creatures are the larger one.

## 6. Against R27-C

- **Done:** #2 (red now belongs to warnings; it created the gold-amber clash in change 2 above), #4 (no aggregate labels, no stamped cones), #7 (the death diagram is large and inked).
- **Partly done:** #1 (near-black olive and a rust-bone Warden, but still flat card masks, and the Warden's glow still reads as a snout lamp), #3 (title and selector are redrawn in style, but the in-play shield still rides the shoulder), #5 (practicals exist but are weak).
- **Not done:** #6 (the arena centres are still bare slabs).
- **Its scores:** I agree with them for final22. For final23 I keep them, except ability effects at 6: Cryo, Whiteout and Meltdown, which it didn't fully sample, pull it down. I raise cohesion to 6.5 for the unified title and selector.
