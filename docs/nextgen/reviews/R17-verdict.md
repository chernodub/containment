Calibration: US$10, "the interface and the first junction look ready to sell; the twenty-room campaign looks like a
budget commercial indie" (Astra visual); "solid commercial-indie quality in combat and exceeds it in rig design; the
biggest gap to premium is reading the next attacker while carrying out your own attack" (Astra play); "a polished
early-access indie with an AA-grade interface wrapped around prototype-grade actors and floors", US$12–15 (Opus).

**Honest reading.** One thing moved for real: the marine. Both visual reviewers now say he is one character from
title to selector to play (Opus marks it done for the first time; Astra's first impression rose 0.8). Everything
else is within half a point in either direction. Play did not move. Seven is still the ceiling nothing crosses
except the interface and the rig kits. The game is a solid indie; it is not premium.

What the three verdicts agree on, in order of weight:
1. **The player's own area effects hide the enemy whose next move matters.** Named first by both Astra reviewers and
   third by Opus, always with the same frame: Pyro in Diagnostic Gallery (fire, black-orange cracks and repeated tick
   numbers over the pack). Orange fire also swallows red hostile markers.
2. **Creatures still have no face and no hard surface.** Astra: the Stalker loses its attacking end, the Brute's blade
   has a stronger contour than its jaw, the Matron's facing is ambiguous, the Warden's armour "leads to a head I
   struggle to locate". Opus: "modelled like plush". The angular plates reached only the Brute's chest and the bosses.
3. **Floors and off-frame incidents.** Opus's only "not done". Shielding Gate, Breached Loading Bay and Freight changed
   too little at fighting distance; room centres "reduce to interchangeable grilles"; warm light pools read as
   smudges; ship conditions look like interface graphics laid on the deck.
4. **Real faults found in the traces** (play reviewer): the plasma and rocket lessons retire when the prompt appears
   (zero plasma shots in all six Passenger Vault traces); self-inflicted rocket splash is logged and shown as
   "scripted" damage; Manual Control Chamber lets three lunges cross at once (the one campaign death; 213 lost by the
   Breacher that survived); the Warden's punish window is buried under its escort's next attack; the route promise is
   silent for nine rooms.
5. **Bodies approaching from below** still vanish under the dock or the frame edge (Armory Cryo fight).
6. **Effects and motion that stay subtle:** Railbreaker and Arc endpoints, Meltdown; marine brake and recoil barely
   visible in contact poses; visor lost its dark lower edge.
7. **Four rendering languages in interface art** (Opus): in-game toon, sketch boon cards, blurry route crops, soft
   title creatures.
