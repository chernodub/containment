## 2. Calibration

**I would pay US$9.99 on these images.** The interface and the combat-warning system look ready for a commercial indie release. The cast is what holds the game below that level overall. In the Diagnostic Gallery storyboard I understand the room change, the danger and the clearance immediately. At the decisive moment I can describe the orange floor effect more confidently than the bodies inside it. Against *Hades*, I miss being able to anticipate a heavy’s next move from its balance alone. Against *The Ascent*, I miss being able to identify a place from the small piece of floor where I happen to be fighting. Motion scores reflect the photographed poses and their order. ## 3. Ten most damaging defects

1. **The Warden has a headlight for a face.** Its light is easy to aim at, but the circular white core fills the head and erases the gap between the jaws. Even a successful wall stun gives me little sense of having hurt an animal. Recess the glow behind an upper and lower jaw, leave a dark crescent between them, and expose the near hinge in the stun pose. [Lineup][heavy] · [Stun][stun]
2. **The Brute has a believable back and unconvincing weight-bearing limbs.** I can follow the curved bands over the shoulder, then the near limb ends in a padded club and the far support in a narrow peg. Repetition makes an ordinary pack look like identical toys. Define two planted knuckles and a wrist crease, then pose the rib cage above the planted hand at mid-stride. [Walk][walk] · [Heavies][heavy]
3. **The Crawler’s attack disappears at contact.** I can find its pink spine on purple deck. When the hook reaches a white boot inside a red fan, the body, target and attack marker meet in a few pixels. This teaches the footprint more effectively than the enemy. Swing one hook outside the boot outline and turn a short bone-coloured cutting edge toward the camera during the load. [Boots][boots] · [Loaded crowd][load]
4. **Incidents fall out of the fight.** Passenger Vault loses much of the berth train to the edge of its incident frame. The reactor is barely present in the native Overload fight. A player who arrives late in either shot has to consult the HUD. Extend a recognizable consequence into the lane, such as a pod-width drag scar or a scorched feeder with raised, broken ends. [Incidents][inc] · [Campaign fights][fights]
5. **Whiteout still camouflages its victims.** The bare channels restore the floor, but a pale flying forearm, an ice shard and a frozen small enemy have much the same edge and value. I lose targets while choosing a follow-up. Knock back the snow immediately behind each body and put one sharp frost highlight on the struck shoulder or hook. [Whiteout peak][white] · [After 300 ms][white3]
6. **The Carrier attracts attention out of proportion to its action.** Broad pink patches on a waiting Carrier draw my eye before the marine or an attacking neighbour. Its legs disappear while its back remains almost fluorescent. Narrow the static seams, retain a readable dark body edge and reserve the broad pink pulse for priming a pod. [Checkpoint crowd][crowd] · [Carrier walk][carrier]
7. **The Brute’s release and recovery have almost the same reach.** The head drops, but the fist still occupies the end of the attack lane. A player deciding when to close must rely on the recovery indicator. Plant the fist on impact, roll the shoulder beyond it, then open daylight between the retreating hand and the head. [Five poses][swing] · [Recovery indicator][load]
8. **The four rigs lose too much of their shape when they aim.** The Breacher’s shield reads broadly now, but can seem suspended above his shoulder. Cryo and Pyro both present a gun, a round shoulder and two projections behind the head. Bring the shield grip into silhouette, pull Cryo’s cylinders into one high-backed assembly and keep Pyro’s exhausts low and widely spaced. [Selector and combat crops][rig] · [Cryo silhouette][tank]
9. **Fire conveys the area hit more strongly than what happened inside it.** The opening fork is forceful. In the native burn frame the brightest sustained orange sits on the pool’s right edge, away from the small creatures in the centre. Move the last hot tongues onto the recoiling bodies and leave a dimmer broken residue on the deck. [Flashfire pair][flame] · [Native burn][burn]
10. **The final crossing still lacks a convincing first refuge.** I can follow the thin preview line back to its source. At the marine’s end, arrows, pool of light and deck edges coincide, and I cannot point to the face that would stop the shot. Give that face visible thickness and terminate the tracer on its exposed side with a brief, localized spark. [Current fork][fork] · [Previous fork][oldfork]

## 4. Five things to preserve

1. **The filled imminent attack.** The hot rim and translucent fan tell me which way to step even on ice. The floor and the marine’s feet remain visible through it. [Cryo Overdrive][cryo] · [Mixed crowd][crowd]
2. **The marine’s visor and local light.** A pale chevron, dark gun and small lit patch of deck let me reacquire the player across twenty rooms without searching the HUD. [Rigs][rig] · [Campaign fights][fights]
3. **The Matron’s abdomen and opening pods.** The same striped organ that identifies the mother explains the little open cups on the floor. The encounter tells a story without a caption. [Pod countdown and opening][pods]
4. **The illustrated choices.** “Plate-breaker route” and “Brood route” put recognizably different opponents above the decision. The boon illustrations likewise show the result of a choice before commitment. [Route and boon cards][cards]
5. **The ending’s restraint.** The broken ship occupies the frame, the warm light gives the eye somewhere to rest, and “All aboard lost / New Earth warned” lands before the statistics. [Ending, lower-left panel][end]

## 8. Five investments for one month, in order

1. **Finish one heavy from the ground contact to the mouth.** Give the common Brute a planted hand, loaded shoulder and visible wrist, then build the Warden’s hinged jaw around a recessed throat. Approve them side by side in gray at fighting size. These two animals appear in crowds, route cards and boss encounters. [Heavies][heavy] · [Walk][walk] · [Routes][cards]
2. **Animate the three moments a player can act on.** Give the Crawler a clearly extended hook, the Brute an unmistakably spent arm and the Warden a jaw that opens toward the player after collision. Preserve the existing red footprint as a second cue. [Boots][boots] · [Brute poses][swing] · [Stun][stun]
3. **Make hit aftermath identify the victim.** Begin with frozen small enemies inside Whiteout and burning enemies in Awakening Bay. Move the sharpest glint with a struck joint and leave a darker gap around each silhouette. Reduce the resting Carrier glow at the same time. [Whiteout aftermath][white3] · [Native burn][burn] · [Checkpoint crowd][crowd]
4. **Carry three accidents through their playable lanes.** Choose a bent barrier and torn anchors for Checkpoint, a scorched branching feed for Transmission and a displaced restraint with dragged cargo for Freight. Each should remain identifiable when the source is outside the camera. [Incidents][inc] · [Campaign fights][fights]
5. **Bring the selector’s equipment into the combat silhouette.** Fit Breacher’s shield to a visible forearm, separate Cryo’s high tank profile from Pyro’s low exhausts and use a common armour highlight on the portrait and game model. The visor and shotgun recoil already give the two views a strong point of continuity. [Rigs][rig] · [Firing pair][breach] · [Shotgun][gun]
