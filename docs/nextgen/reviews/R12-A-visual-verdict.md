## 2. Calibration

This is a **polished prototype approaching a modest commercial indie presentation**, not a game approaching the best premium indie or AA visual standard. Against my reference points of *Hades*, *Curse of the Dead Gods* and *The Ascent*, it lacks authored scene identity, expressive attack posing and persuasive material treatment—not merely polygon count. The clear interface is ahead of the world and creatures; the first minute does not sustain the title's promise. I would try a demo and consider **US$8–12 if the actual combat were excellent**. These images alone would not persuade me to pay US$20–30. That is personal willingness to pay, not market-price research. (`title.png`; `onboarding-awakening-bay.png`; `twenty-calm.png`; `play/breacher/storyboard-armory.png`)

## 3. Ten most damaging defects, ranked

1. **The ship's rooms are differentiated more by inventory than by dramatic composition.**
   **Where:** `twenty-calm.png`, particularly Residential Gallery, Coolant Plant, Infested Workshop and Manual Control Chamber; `review480/rooms--combat-calm-infested-workshop.png`.
   **Why it matters:** The player sees a large amount of familiar floor before seeing anything that explains what happened here. Room names promise a narrative escalation that the visible spaces only weakly deliver.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Paint over five representative gameplay views. Give each one a dominant incident with a clear large shape: breached pod bank, interrupted evacuation, frozen leak, machinery consumed by brood mass, burned manual-control station. Carry its damage, stains and displaced objects into the surrounding floor without filling movement lanes with obstacles.

2. **The strongest powers share too much of the same visual climax.**
   **Where:** `review480/abilities--v2-whiteout-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-04.png`, `review480/abilities--v2-meltdown-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-04.png`, `review480/abilities--v2-overclock-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-04.png`; `abilities/v2-napalm-charge-relay-racks-relay-racks-04.png`.
   **Why it matters:** Whiteout, Meltdown and Overclock become concentric rings with different colours; Napalm's dominant shape is a dark circular texture stamp. These are functional indicators, not sufficiently different fantasies to sell four rigs.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Storyboard a separate anticipation, impact and residue for each overdrive. Make Whiteout crystallize outward, Meltdown rupture with concentrated heat, and Overclock surge through equipment and connected targets. Keep accurate area boundaries underneath, but break up the cosmetic fire interior and add localized vertical tongues instead of an opaque disk.

3. **The marine loses its material identity in gameplay.**
   **Where:** `rig-select.png`; `onboarding-awakening-bay.png`; `native-heavy/cryo-play-r6-armory-040.png`.
   **Why it matters:** The portrait sells rounded, layered armour; the gameplay body becomes pale patches enclosed by hard black edges, with a comparatively dark weapon. The player's most important asset looks less finished than its promotional image. This is an appearance finding, not a diagnosis of actual transparency.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Rebalance armour into three broad value groups, retain a dark under-suit, preserve a readable helmet/shoulder separation and put one controlled highlight along the weapon. Reduce internal outline weight before adding texture detail. Review those changes at the gameplay camera on both pale and dark floors.

4. **Boss-class creatures do not command a distinct visual tier.**
   **Where:** `enemy-variants/g12-native-brute-warden-awakening-bay-sprite.png`; `enemy-variants/g12-native-carrier-matron-awakening-bay-sprite.png`; `native-heavy/play-r6-armory-015.png`.
   **Why it matters:** Warden and Matron read primarily as highlighted family variants. Their warning graphics announce more importance than their bodies do, wasting the game's most obvious opportunities for memorable enemies.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Give Warden a conspicuous asymmetric defensive mass and a uniquely readable attack apparatus; give Matron an expanded, burdened brood structure with an obvious opening. Establish a recognizable silhouette without outline, health bar or colour coding before refining surfaces.

5. **The creature families have roles, but insufficient biological specificity.**
   **Where:** `motion/carrier-overview.png`; `enemies/k7-windup-spitter-awakening-bay-sprite.png`; `enemies/m10-melee-stalker-awakening-bay-sprite.png`; `native-heavy/cryo-play-r6-armory-040.png`.
   **Why it matters:** The carrier's pink mass feels ornamental; the spitter is identified mainly by its green sac; the stalker is a small dark angular shape. The cast is readable as game pieces before it is convincing as an alien species.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Strengthen carrier abdominal weight and leg support, connect the spitter sac visibly to a directional mouth, and widen the stalker's key shoulder/scythe silhouette. Separate shell and soft tissue through broad values and roughness, not more fine black lines.

6. **Combat overlays can replace the anatomy they are meant to explain.**
   **Where:** `champion-mechanics/g2-champions-armory-sprite.png`; `compare/final7/compare-heavy--combat-heavy-armory-01.png`; `review480/builds--v2-full-cryo-armory-armory-04.png`; `review480/builds--v2-full-pyro-armory-armory-04.png`.
   **Why it matters:** Filled melee sectors help individually, but overlapping sectors, highlighted bodies, frost, rings and damage numbers compete for the same small region. Some highlighted enemies lose surface structure; full-build frames also push threats toward the lower HUD.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Set an explicit visual priority: imminent hostile boundary, actor body, player ability boundary, cosmetic particles, damage text. Preserve opaque creature cores; lower cosmetic density near actors; merge redundant status markers and prevent nonessential particles from covering imminent warnings. Do not weaken telegraph accuracy.

7. **Action coverage exceeds action expressiveness.**
   **Where:** `motion/marine-overview.png`; `motion/brute-overview.png`; `motion/crawler-overview.png`; `motion/carrier-overview.png`.
   **Why it matters:** Multiple actions are present, but the marine often keeps the same gun-bearing upper-body shape, and several creature actions vary limbs more than overall silhouette. That limits the impression of effort and attack identity. The sheets do not establish foot sliding or bad timing.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Rework four essential poses per actor: preparation, commitment, contact and recovery. Start with marine heavy fire/cast, brute strike and stalker lunge; exaggerate shoulder/pelvis opposition and body compression while preserving aiming clarity. Then evaluate timed playback before changing durations.

8. **The reward and decision screens feel like administration.**
   **Where:** `review480/starting-boon.png`; `review480/augment-draft.png`; `review480/shop.png`; `review480/route.png`; `review480/pause-loadout-augments.png`.
   **Why it matters:** Boon props are a useful start, but repeated pedestals, dense labels and near-empty route cards do little to create anticipation. The shop's long text list has no strong visual object of desire.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Give each boon a distinct large silhouette and one prominent behaviour sentence. Add a compact before/after combat diagram for transformative upgrades. Put destination imagery or a simple route vignette in route cards; reduce their height if no meaningful image is available. Give weapons recognizable thumbnails in the shop.

9. **Quiet-room lighting still looks constructed from graphic patches.**
   **Where:** `review480/rooms--combat-calm-communal-atrium.png`; `review480/rooms--combat-calm-crew-checkpoint.png`; `review480/rooms--combat-calm-relay-racks.png`.
   **Why it matters:** Hard pale wedges, soft isolated light spots and broad uniform floor values expose the lighting treatment. The result lacks a convincing relationship between practical lights, nearby surfaces and room atmosphere.
   **Tomorrow's fix:** Match each major pool to a visible source, soften or break unjustified wedge boundaries, and concentrate contrast around one landmark. Keep secondary floor illumination broad and quiet; avoid restoring oversized overhead fixtures.

10. **Framing is improved but not sufficiently evidenced at the hardest boundaries.**
    **Where:** `twenty-kiting.png` has no Manual Control combat cell; `review480/builds--v2-full-cryo-armory-armory-04.png` and `review480/builds--v2-full-pyro-armory-armory-04.png` show actor/debris overlap and lower-edge crowding. `DEFECTS.md` attributes the missing cell to the warning quad crossing the HUD-safe region and cites `rooms/g6-kiting-manual-control-chamber-failure.png`.
    **Why it matters:** A warning extending into HUD space risks hiding the information that makes a hit feel fair. Staged debris overlap also weakens the hero silhouette, though it does not prove a collision defect.
    **Tomorrow's fix:** Reproduce the documented Manual Control warning placement and fit the whole visible warning into the combat-safe composition. Audit foreground/debris fading around the marine and reserve lower-screen threat space. I did not open the failure PNG; that specific failure is capture-agent evidence, not an independently reproduced bug.

## 4. Five things worth preserving

1. **Clear interface hierarchy and keyboard cues.** The title, difficulty choices and rig diagrams explain where to act without decorative confusion. (`title.png`; `difficulty-selection.png`; `rig-select.png`)
2. **Protected combat numerals and directional danger.** Health, ammunition and cooldowns remain readable, and the native charge lane communicates a committed threat immediately. (`native-heavy/play-r6-armory-015.png`; `native-heavy/cryo-play-r6-armory-040.png`)
3. **Landmarks that genuinely organize space.** The Armory's curved installation, the service shaft's depth and the Overload Floor's reactor give the ship useful large-scale structure. (`twenty-calm.png`, Armory/Service Shaft Landing/Overload Floor cells)
4. **Elemental differentiation without indiscriminate bloom.** Glacier has physical volume, Cryo Nova now suggests a frozen surface, and Railbreaker gives a clear directional event. Preserve these readable foundations. (`review480/abilities--v2-glacier-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-04.png`; `compare/final7/abilities--v2-cryo-nova-awakening-bay-04.png`; `review480/builds--v2-full-breacher-armory-armory-04.png`)
5. **An ending-specific image.** The abandoned transmission equipment and departing signal give the ending a subject beyond another character portrait. It still needs stronger staging, but this is the right kind of authored closure. (`review480/complete-fixture.png`)

## 8. One month: five highest-return investments

1. **Author five signature room incidents, then propagate their visual rules.** Prioritize the opening, residential section, infestation transition, manual-control climax and reactor. One environment artist with lighting support can replace generic floor-led compositions with clear story images without rebuilding all twenty rooms. (`twenty-calm.png`; `review480/rooms--combat-calm-infested-workshop.png`)
2. **Give the four rigs different climaxes and disciplined effect priority.** Rework overdrives, Napalm and frost accumulation; keep strong hostile boundaries and visible actor cores. This improves both screenshots and the player's ability to understand a full build. (`review480/builds--v2-full-cryo-armory-armory-04.png`; `review480/builds--v2-full-pyro-armory-armory-04.png`; `review480/builds--v2-full-arc-armory-armory-04.png`)
3. **Finish the marine as a gameplay asset.** Pair a character artist and animator for armour values, weapon readability and distinct heavy-fire/cast poses. Check the documented warning/framing edge cases during the same actual-camera review. (`rig-select.png`; `native-heavy/cryo-play-r6-armory-040.png`; `motion/marine-overview.png`; `DEFECTS.md`)
4. **Make Warden and Matron memorable, then strengthen the weakest family silhouettes.** Spend on large body design and four convincing action poses before skin detail or more variants; prioritize Stalker and Carrier next. (Both `enemy-variants/g12-native-*-awakening-bay-sprite.png` boss sheets cited above; `enemies/m10-melee-stalker-awakening-bay-sprite.png`; `motion/carrier-overview.png`)
5. **Make rewards desirable while preserving the UI structure.** Commission distinctive boon/weapon imagery, one meaningful preview per transformative upgrade and compact destination vignettes. Improve the ending scene's scale and lighting after those recurring screens. (`review480/starting-boon.png`; `review480/augment-draft.png`; `review480/shop.png`; `review480/route.png`; `review480/complete-fixture.png`)
