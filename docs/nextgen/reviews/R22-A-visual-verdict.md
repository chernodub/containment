## 2. What I would pay

**$10 US.** I would buy this as a small commercial action roguelike. I can see an interesting fight in the Armory storyboard alone. The circular installation gives me a way to orient myself, a ranged shot crosses the room, and the marine has a readable escape from a melee sweep. Against *Hades*, I miss the ability to predict a threat from the actor's weight shift. Against *The Ascent*, I miss being able to recognise a neighbourhood from the patch of floor under a fight. The rig selector is already convincing at a premium price. The twenty campaign frames together put the game a tier below that. Motion judgments here concern sampled poses and sequences. ## 3. Ten defects, in priority order

1. **Heavy enemies obscure the player at contact.** In the Armory pile-up a sliver of yellow gear and part of a foot ring are practically all that locate the marine. A telegraph is only actionable if I know where my feet are. Keep the Warden's scale and reveal the marine's visor and planted-foot silhouette through the overlapping portion of its shell. [frames 9](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/review-sheets/frames-09.png) · [Armory / Cryo](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/twenty-native.png)
2. **Dark anatomy collapses inside a good outer contour.** In the greyscale lineup the Warden has an enormous readable shoulder and very little readable head. The regular Brute's weapon arm joins its belly. Paint one broad lit head plane and one continuous striking edge per family, and keep the deepest shadow at the attachment between the two. [Colour, value and silhouette](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/heavies.png) · [frames 6](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/review-sheets/frames-06.png)
3. **Encounter centres lose the room's story.** The Armory incident view reduces its great carousel to a cable end. The shaft landing reduces its hoist to overhead steel and bare deck. A player kiting at centre misses the artwork. Carry a recognisable piece of the machine across the floor, such as an ammunition feed or a cable under tension, and light its origin and termination together. [Spawn views](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/twenty-calm.png) · [Incident views](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/twenty-incident.png) · [Campaign fights](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/twenty-native.png)

On the lighter Checkpoint lane both are much easier to locate. Give the Stalker a pale leading blade and the Crawler a broader forward claw, then tune those two edges against the darkest floor. ## 4. Five things to preserve

1. **The marine's value contrast.** A pale helmet over dark joints stays easy to relocate on both warm freight decking and cold relay steel. Preserve that contrast when improving overlap with the boss. [Campaign fights](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/twenty-native.png)
2. **The order of combat warnings.** The filled sector gives the eye a first decision, the lighter outlines describe what follows, and Cryo leaves the active sector legible through the snow. [frames 1](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/review-sheets/frames-01.png) · [frames 8](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/review-sheets/frames-08.png)
3. **Physical consequences of a hit.** The rail blast pitches two bodies off their feet. The Crawler folds differently when frozen and when thrown by force. These are better rewards for using a weapon than a larger damage number. [Rail impact](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/overdrive/gallery-overdrive-breacher-crew-checkpoint-03.png) · [Crawler reactions](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/motion/crawler-contact-1.png)
4. **The first route choice and its payoff.** The two enemy illustrations make Plate-breaker and Brood a decision about what to fight. The arrival card recalls that consequence inside the destination room. [screens 6](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/review-sheets/screens-06.png) · [frames 4](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final17/review-sheets/frames-04.png)
