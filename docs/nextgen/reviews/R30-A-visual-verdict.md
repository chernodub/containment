## 2. What I would pay

**US$12 on visual evidence.** I would buy this as a small commercial action roguelike. The cargo choice shows why. In one picture I understand a rush, in the other I see crossing lines of fire, and I can choose which problem to take into the next room. The gap to *Hades* is clearest in a Brute’s ordinary stride, where I should feel the animal’s weight before it attacks. The gap to *The Ascent* is clearest on the far bank of the reactor, where the geometry that might save me is hard to distinguish from the deck. This judgment covers captured play and pose sequences. Timing, responsiveness and performance were outside the review. [Cargo choice][choice] · [Stride][walk] · [Final campaign storyboard][story]

## 8. Five investments for one month

1. **Spend the first week on one Brute walking past a fixed deck seam.** Pair the animator with the creature artist. Make the palm plant, breast travel and finger release convincing in the same view, then carry that mechanics work to the elite and Warden. It pays off every time a heavy enters a room. [Stride][walk] · [Native fights][fight]
2. **Finish the contact between a small attacker, its warning and the marine.** Start at the exact Swarm Junction gun overlap and the Interlock edge. Shape the Crawler’s head and move the first bright warning segment until the same glance identifies the attacker and the escape. [Boots][boots] · [Ordered pack][ordered] · [Interlock][edge]
3. **Build the final approach and three other fighting lanes end to end.** Start on the dangerous side of the reactor shelter and work backward from the face that stops the shot. Give Passenger, Checkpoint and Transmission similarly traceable pieces of ship hardware through both banks. [Refuge impact][bank] · [Passenger][pc] · [Checkpoint][cc] · [Transmission][tc]
4. **Give each expensive power one unmistakable victim reaction.** Start with the last enemy on Meltdown’s edge, a small enemy freezing inside Whiteout and the final link of an Arc chain. Use Railbreaker’s displaced bodies as the standard for how much information a single frame can carry. [Heat][heat] · [Ice][white] · [Arc][arc] · [Railbreaker][rail]
5. **Do a native-size paint and lighting pass on the recurring hero and boss shapes.** Start with the Warden tongue, elite crown, heavy wrist and the negative space under Arc’s raised arm. Reuse those corrected shapes in the title and route art. These are the details players see both on the store page and at close range.
