## 6. What the genre leaders deliver better

1. **A safe next move the player can name before pressing dash.** *Enter the Gungeon* is my benchmark for seeing an incoming pattern and its gap in the same glance. Containment’s final bank often sells the threat first and reveals the foothold on impact.
2. **A hit that identifies its author.** In *Hades*, a committed body pose can tell me which opponent to punish while I am already turning. Here the red footprint can remain clear even when its owner disappears under the marine.
3. **A fight that changes tempo when I solve it.** A guardian kill should buy a breath, a lane or a changed wave the player immediately feels. A 52-second continuation spends that moment.
4. **An upgrade with an unmistakable first use and a later cost.** The ice runway and advancing barricade deserve fights that make the player choose a different route. The supply drawings currently demonstrate that change more reliably than the first native casts.
5. **A journey whose choices reappear.** Booking the Coolant guardian delivers anticipation and recognition several rooms apart. The two order junctions deserve a consequence of comparable quality.

## 8. Five investments for one month

1. **Make Atrium a demonstration and Manual Control a decision.** Give the second room two attractive firing positions with different costs. Watch fresh desktop players once without coaching. Ask which escorts they stopped and where they would stand next time. A player who deliberately allows one through for a better shot has learned more than a bot that closes every grille.
