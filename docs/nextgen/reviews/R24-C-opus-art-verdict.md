# R24-C — art-director second opinion (Claude Opus, 2026-11-25, gallery final19)

Evidence: 44 images; I did not run the game. Within budget I cut 2 fights, 2 incident frames, Whiteout, the windup crowd, 1 overlap frame and 1 conditions frame. The windup sprites were too small to judge.

## 1. Scores

- **First impression: 6.** The title's type and layout are premium, and the back-lit creature staging is moody. But the PBR marine with the "07" dome is not the marine you play (`title.png`).
- **Interface: 7.5.** One disciplined system. The death card names the cause and the fix, and the boon diagrams are good. Weak spots: the route-card Warden is a green blob carrying a tan sack, and the boon-card creatures are still brown (`run15-condition-route.png`, `augment-draft.png`).
- **Combat readability: 6.** The red telegraph grammar is excellent (`bosses/…armory-charge-windup`). But jade Brutes are now the most saturated mass in most frames (`twenty-kiting.png`), and the small families form a candy-coloured confetti of orange, pink, lime, violet and periwinkle.
- **Environments: 6.** Twenty distinct grades and a good ink line. The arena centres are still empty slate plates under one spotlight, and the landmarks are dim (`rooms--incident-facing-coolant-plant`).
- **Ability effects: 6.** Railbreaker, Meltdown and amber Pyro fire read well. Cryo Nova and Seismic Slam are hairline rings at 480×270, and I cannot find Overload's peak (`abilities--v2-overload-…-03`).
- **Characters and creatures: 4.5.** The marine is a 6.5: a clean silhouette with the rig colour on the sphere. The creatures are a 3.5. The jade is flat and unlit, with no form value, so they look like papercraft cut-outs. The Warden's pale "mark" is a beige shell covering a third of its body and reads as unpainted geometry (`bosses/ktx2-warden-armory-charge-windup.png`).
- **Cohesion: 5.** There are three languages: PBR on the title and selector, lit toon for the marine and the rooms, and unlit flat stickers for the creatures.

## 2. Tier

Polished early-access indie: AA menus over prototype creatures. I would pay $12 today; $20 after changes 1–4.

## 3. Seven changes, ranked

1. **Form-light the creatures** (`heavies.png`, native Warden). Run a 3-step cel ramp from the room's key light: shadow at 18% value, plane at 35%, a 1–2 px cool rim. The promised "raking light" is not visible in any frame.
2. **Darken and desaturate the jade, and shrink the pale mark** (`twenty-kiting.png`, `heavies.png`). Drop body chroma about 30% and value about 15%, so Brutes sit under the lit marine. Limit the mark to 8–10% of the body area: a skull plate or a tusk, not a shell. The Brute rebuild is in work; the Warden needs the same pass.
3. **One species base for the small families** (`crowds/…crew-checkpoint-01.png`). Give them a shared dark carapace and keep the family hue only in marks, eyes and stripes. Today they read as five unrelated toy sets.
4. **Make the abilities read at gameplay scale** (`abilities--v2-cryo-nova-03`, `-seismic-slam-03`, `-overload-03`). Fill the ground decal under each ring at 25% opacity and add a 2-frame white core flash. Give Overload branching bolts and a violet scorch.
5. **Arena floor storytelling** (`twenty-arena-calm.png`). In the 4 m centre, add grates, cable runs, scorch and fluid stains, contact AO at prop bases, and a second practical light pool, so each landmark emits light and ranks third.
6. **One marine** (`title.png` against `rigs-in-play.png`). Re-render the title marine at in-game proportions, with the rig sphere, the chevron visor and the ink line. Drop the "07" dome or carry it into play.
7. **Re-shoot the card art from the game** (`run15-condition-route.png`, `augment-draft.png`). Render the route and boon creatures from current models under the game's lighting.

## 4. Keep

1. The hostile telegraph grammar: red cones, capsules and the "Charging · 1s" pill (`native-heavy/arc-pressure-4`).
2. The UI system: Barlow type, boon before/after diagrams, and the death debrief.
3. Amber player fire. It is clearly separate from hostile red now (`overlap--…pyro-03`).

## 5. Value hierarchy, identity, the decision

The hierarchy is not complete. In calm and windup frames the marine is lit first. In fights the Brute family now beats him: big, saturated, mid-value green masses take the eye before his small grey-and-gold figure (`twenty-kiting.png`, the native crowd frame). Landmarks come third only in the reactor, workshop and annulus rooms. The marine's identity holds from the selector to play; the chevron visor reads in the motion strips and barely at 480. The title marine is still a different, glossy, bulkier figure. The decision holding the picture back most: **creatures are shaded as flat unlit colour fills, while everything around them is lit.** Recolouring them did not fix that; it made them louder stickers.

## 6. Against R23-C

- **Done:** #2 (player fire is amber and separate from hostile red).
- **Partly done:** #3 (the visor chevron is there; the pack is still the marine's biggest shape), #5 (the Warden has a bright maw; it is still a lump with a shell), #6 (violet bolts in native Arc frames; no visible Overload peak at 480) and #7 (the title creatures are the game's own, and the route cards now have room backdrops; the art itself is stale).
- **Not done:** #1 (creature form light: the recolour changed only the hue) and #4 (arena floors).
- **Its scores:** I agree with its scores for final18, except creatures: the jade recolour without form light makes them read worse, so I score 4.5, not 5.5.
