# R15-C — art-director second opinion (Claude Opus, 2026-11-20, gallery final10)

Evidence: 44 images from `final10/`. I looked at 6 screens, 3 contact sheets, 30 frames at 480×270, 3 native-size frames and 2 motion sheets. I took five heavy-combat rooms instead of six, to stay within the image budget; `twenty-heavy.png` covers the rest. I did not run the game.

## 1. Scores

- **First impression: 6.** The title has strong type and composition. Against it, the creatures are untextured grey clay and the background is abstract shards (`title.png`).
- **Interface: 7.5.** The system is clean and consistent: rig diagrams, illustrated boon cards and the route previews. Two weaknesses: the shop icons are toy blocks, and the death card still says "1 damage" beside a 150-damage source (`onb14-death-stalker.png`).
- **Combat readability: 5.5.** One-on-one telegraphs are crisp (`enemies--…-stalker-attack-…-03`). At density, a solid red wedge sits on the marine inside a carpet of identical Brutes (`rooms--combat-heavy-armory-02`, `twenty-heavy.png`).
- **Environments: 5.5.** All twenty rooms have their own grade, but most of every frame is empty mid-grey tile. Crew Checkpoint is lit flat (`rooms/combat-calm-diagnostic-gallery.png`, `abilities--…-crew-checkpoint-03`).
- **Ability effects: 6.** Railbreaker, Cryo Nova, Whiteout, Seismic Slam and Pyro's floor cracks land. Meltdown, Overload and Napalm are thin rings or lines (`abilities--v2-meltdown-…-03`).
- **Characters and creatures: 5.** Warden and Matron have real silhouettes. The marine is ochre and grey camouflage blotches (`motion/marine-run-45.png`). The small families shrink to dark specks at 480, and up close the creatures smear into grey blobs (`native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`).
- **Cohesion: 6.** The UI and the ink-line toon style agree. The title marine and the creatures are a different rendering from the in-game ones.

## 2. Tier

This is a competent early-access indie: the UI is commercial, but the actors and crowd fights are not. I would pay $12–15.

## 3. Seven changes, ranked

1. **The Brute carpet** (`twenty-heavy.png`, `rooms--combat-heavy-coolant-plant-02`). The Brutes' near-white wing blades are their brightest value and they compete with the marine. Drop the blades to about 50% value and keep a cool rim. Vary scale ±10% and the idle phase. Seed every wave with other families so the crowd has more than one silhouette.
2. **Telegraphs under the hero** (`rooms--combat-heavy-armory-02`, `native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`). Use a 15–20% fill with a bright leading edge, and cut out a 0.6 m radius around the marine. Draw the marine last, with a 2px warm outline.
3. **Small families vanish** (`enemies--…-crawler-attack-…-03`, `elite-pairs--…-stalker`). Scale Crawlers and Spitters up 25%, give each family one saturated emissive accent, and give elites a gold crest, 1.3× scale and a ground halo.
4. **Landmarks at the frame edge** (`incident-matched--…-overload-floor`, `…-swarm-junction`). Move each landmark into the upper third, clear of the objective card, and make it the room's brightest practical light at about 2× floor luminance.
5. **Empty floors** (`rooms/combat-calm-diagnostic-gallery.png`). Lower the tile value 15%. Add grime, cable runs and drag decals leading to the landmark, plus contact AO at the walls and pooled practical lights per zone.
6. **Weak area abilities** (`abilities--v2-meltdown-…-03`, `…overload-…-03`, `…napalm-charge-…-03`). Give each one a white-hot filled core for 3 frames, then a thick shape, then a 2 s scorch or charge decal with vertical embers or sparks.
7. **Creature material** (`native-heavy/pyro-pressure-1.png`, `motion/stalker-attack.png`, `title.png`). Paint three value bands: dark chitin, mid flesh and one saturated accent. Remove the smeared hit dissolve, and bring the title creatures up to the same finish.

## 4. Keep

1. The UI language: Barlow, the before/after diagrams and the illustrated boon cards (`rig-breacher-quick.png`, `augment-draft.png`).
2. Twenty distinct room grades with ink-lined props (`twenty-incident.png`).
3. The red-only hostile telegraphs, plus Railbreaker and Cryo Nova (`abilities--v2-railbreaker-…-03`).

## 5. Value hierarchy and identity

The hierarchy works only in calm frames. There the ship is dark, the marine stands in its own pool of light, and the creatures sit below it. Under pressure it inverts. The Brute blades and the red wedges outshine the marine, and the small families sink into the floor. The landmark tier is still missing: reactors and wrecks are cropped at the top edge and left unlit. The marine keeps its shape from title to play but not its finish. The title shows a smooth white and yellow "07" trooper with a cyan visor. In play it is posterised ochre and grey camouflage that turns blue on Cryo (`native-heavy/cryo-pressure-2.png`). The decision holding the picture back most is **where the creatures' value sits**. Their bodies share the floor's value band, while their brightest value is on non-essential blades. Put the creatures' value mass one step below the marine and their accents on the heads and weak points.

## 6. Against R14-C

- **Done:** none fully.
- **Partly done:** #1, one marine (more title yellow, but still blotched). #3, Brute value and scale (bigger, with warm legs, but the brightest value has moved from the head highlight to the blades). #6, area-ability cores (Whiteout and Seismic Slam gained mass; Meltdown is still a ring). #7, player effects yield (the pyro overlap now keeps the hostile markers visible).
- **Not done:** #2, telegraphs off the hero. #4, landmarks in frame. #5, breaking up the floor.
- **Its scores:** I agree within half a point. I give characters 5 instead of 4.5 because the Brutes are now larger and warmer, and ability effects 6 instead of 6.5 because Meltdown, Overload and Napalm are still weak.
