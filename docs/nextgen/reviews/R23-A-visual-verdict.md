## 2. What I would pay

**$10 US on the visual pitch.** This looks like a small commercial action roguelike with a particularly good interface. I would remember firing through the Armory and taking the long way around the final reactor. Against *Hades*, I miss the moment when a creature's change of stance alone tells me what it will do. Against *The Ascent*, I miss recognising a place from what lies immediately under a character's feet. Those differences show up throughout a run. My motion score concerns the poses and sequences visible in the gallery. ## 3. Ten defects, ranked by damage to the experience

1. **The common Brute has no convincing face at fighting distance.** The hook and feet survive; the middle is a knot of olive, grey and black. I see an obstacle before I see an animal choosing to swing. Give the brow and jaw a single sloping plane that points at the target, and open a strip of floor between the belly and cleaver in the loaded pose. Judge it against the marine in the same shot. [Lineup](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final18/heavies.png) · [Loaded Brute](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final18/review-sheets/frames-06.png)
2. **A recovery cue can point at empty floor.** After the Stalker's lunge the pale brackets sit to the marine's right while the animal has crossed to the left. An invitation to punish sends my aim the wrong way. Anchor the brackets to the projected recovering torso through displacement; retain the bracket spacing that reads so well on the stationary Brute. [Stalker recovery](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final18/enemies/value14-stalker-attack-crew-checkpoint-crew-checkpoint-14.png) · [Exposed Brute](/Users/chernodub/.t3/projects/containment-game/artifacts/nextgen/final18/review-sheets/frames-05.png)
3. **The small brood lose their leading edge in low light.** On the purple Junction floor I can count salmon backs, but have trouble pointing to a resting Stalker's blade or an olive Spitter's muzzle. This costs the player preparation time before the red fill appears. Give each a small persistent light plane on the relevant anatomy and carry it into the loaded pose. Resolve the brow, jaw, blade root and loaded arm in one camera-facing pose, then derive the elite's crest and Warden's charge from that anatomy. A successful common enemy improves more frames than a new boss. 2. **Make a small enemy's intention readable before its warning peaks.** Give the Stalker one bright blade face and a held, asymmetric loaded pose. Carry the same treatment to the Spitter muzzle and Crawler claw, and follow the moving actor through recovery. The payoff is being able to choose a target quickly in a dark pack.
