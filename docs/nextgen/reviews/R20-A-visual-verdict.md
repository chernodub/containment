## 2. Commercial calibration

**I’d pay US$12 for this build on its visual showing.** I would trust the team to teach me the game. I can see what killed me, what a route promises, and what freezing a crowd buys me. The gap to *Hades* is clearest when three enemies touch and I have to read the floor to understand their gestures. Against *Ruiner*, the weak frame is the quiet middle of a room, where the deck tells me much less about the people who lived here than the warm light and overturned furniture did at the door. This looks like a modest commercial indie with a few premium-quality moments. Motion judgments concern sampled poses and storyboards. `screen-extra/onb14-death-stalker.png`, `screen-extra/run15-condition-route.png`, `native-heavy/cryo-pressure-4.png`, `twenty-incident.png`, `twenty-native.png`.

## 3. Ten defects, ranked by damage to the experience

1. **Overlapping attacks lose their owners at the last metre.** In loaded Swarm I can pick out the three pale bodies and the dangerous patch, but their red fans cross under the marine. The next dodge becomes a guess about timing. Carry a short bright stroke from each attacking limb to its fan apex and give the earliest strike one final, distinct pose. `windups/loaded-crowd-swarm-junction-swarm-junction.png`, `windups/loaded-crowd-relay-racks-relay-racks.png`.
2. **Small enemies hide their business end.** The Stalker’s blade and Crawler’s legs tuck under coloured backs at this camera angle. Learning the opponent means learning a lane graphic. Open negative space between blade and belly on the loaded keyframe and give the attacking surface one broad light plane. `windups/loaded-{stalker,crawler}-crew-checkpoint-01.png`, `play23/play23-crowd-swarm-junction.png`.
3. **The ship’s identity often goes out of frame during play.** Relay’s racks, the shaft winch and the Armory carousel make strong establishing views. Their arena centres revert to deck markings, so consecutive fights look cheaper than their rooms. Extend a rack bus, winch cable or spent-ammunition trail into each fighting lane as flush dressing. `twenty-calm.png`, `twenty-arena-calm.png`, `twenty-native.png`.
4. **The two final banks undersell the final decision.** One capture gives a small charging tag against an almost black deck, the other puts the marine beside a huge machine. I can see the change of cover more readily than I can identify either bank. Give each a visible terminal face, a conduit to the core and a distinctive charge lamp inside the hold area. `play23/play23-bank-{0,1}-overload-floor.png`.
5. **The off-screen warning meets the busiest piece of HUD.** The Safety-interlock frame has a red lane, a partially visible pale shooter and a tiny red pointer competing with bottom-dock icons. The lane direction survives reduction better than the jaw shape. Reserve a strip just above the dock and anchor a larger two-part marker to the incoming bearing. `play23/play23-edge-safety-interlock-station.png`, `review-sheets/frames-01.png`.
6. **Several Overdrives spend their visual weight away from the victim.** The two “420” flashes float well above Railbreaker’s horizontal beam. Overclock spreads a handsome mesh across empty floor while one target shows a small “7.” The costly button press needs a payoff the eye can connect to the shot. Keep a short displaced-target trail for Railbreaker and make Arc’s successive contacts light each victim’s silhouette. `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-{breacher,arc}-crew-checkpoint-03.png`.
7. **Boss anatomy establishes rank better than it explains the attack.** The Warden’s large plate and the Matron’s raised sac are excellent identifiers. The Warden’s forward contact and the Matron’s release into the nearest pod are harder to trace among rings. Stage a weight shift onto the Warden’s striking side and carry the Matron’s emission visibly from sac to pod. `bosses/ktx2-warden-crew-checkpoint-charge.png`, `bosses/pod-stages/ktx2-matron-crew-checkpoint-pod-opening.png`, `review-sheets/frames-09.png`.
8. **Three rigs sell the same figure.** Breacher, Cryo and Arc share a tucked side stance and a dominant flat shoulder disc. At selection I remember their colours more readily than their equipment. Let the shield-side brace, coolant apparatus and conductor project beyond the body in different directions. Pyro’s wide footing and long gun already show the return. `review-sheets/screens-05.png`, `screens-06.png`.
9. **Warm bodies disappear into sustained fire.** The isolated pool has a readable interior. In native Swarm, orange seams, hit numbers and brown plates occupy the same patch, erasing the legs and facing of the middle creature. Keep a dark pocket beneath each standing victim and move the brightest flame tips into the gaps. `napalm-pair/resting-and-crowded.png`, `play/pyro/storyboard-manual-control-chamber.png`.
10. **Secondary UI text gets the smallest type even when it decides the purchase.** The Weapons tab leaves most of its panel empty while modifiers crowd into a shallow row. Supply prices, benefits and condition copy compete in small cards. Pair a selected weapon or item with one full-size explanation and keep price beside the action. `screen-extra/pause-weapons.png`, `screen-extra/play21-supply.png`, `review-sheets/screens-03.png`.

## 4. Five strengths to preserve

1. **The marine is easy to reacquire.** Even among white ice fragments, the helmet, shoulder and projecting weapon make one directional figure. Preserve that light-dark grouping when enriching the armour. `native-heavy/cryo-pressure-4.png`, `twenty-native.png`.
2. **A single committed attack is remarkably fair to the eye.** In the native Coolant fight the outlined creature, fan apex and outer red edge explain source, reach and escape in one glance. This works on a dark floor with several spectators. `native-heavy/breacher-pressure-2.png`.
3. **The first junction makes a promise the destination keeps.** An armoured beast and a brood-bearing creature illustrate two named routes. The arrival at Coolant says which opponent is coming while leaving the room visible. `screen-extra/run15-condition-route.png`, `review-sheets/frames-03.png`.
4. **The best environmental storytelling comes from physical evidence.** The cracked, reflective coolant spill beneath teeth of ice and the plates scattered beside the Residential table tell different stories at 480 pixels. Keep those relationships between object, damage and residue. `twenty-incident.png`.
5. **The debrief gives a player something specific to do on retry.** Crawler and Stalker deaths have different attack diagrams and different movement advice. The quiet final transmission then gives the campaign a distinct ending image. `screen-extra/onb14-death-{crawler,stalker}.png`, `screen-extra/complete-fixture.png`.

## 8. One month, five investments in order

1. **Put the threat in the attacking body.** Give Crawler and Stalker one readable loaded silhouette each, connect the contact limb to its warning and make waiting creatures brace or circle. Keep the attack budget. This pays off in almost every room, especially where two fans touch. `windups/loaded-crowd-swarm-junction-swarm-junction.png`, `native-heavy/arc-pressure-3.png`.
2. **Make the off-screen approach and the final crossing legible.** Give the shooter marker clean dock clearance, preserve room below the marine for committed attackers, and light each relay’s approach from the terminal outward. These are small interventions in places where the player must act quickly. `play23/play23-edge-safety-interlock-station.png`, `rig-fights/v2-kit-cryo-high-armory-armory-04.png`, `play23/play23-bank-{0,1}-overload-floor.png`.
3. **Carry four room stories through the fighting lane.** Start with Checkpoint’s scanner throat, Gate’s jammed mechanism, Transmission’s signal path and Manual Control’s passenger bank. Give each one recognisable shape, one practical light and a trace of use that remains in view while circling. `twenty-incident.png`, `twenty-arena-calm.png`, `twenty-native.png`.
4. **Spend the next effects pass on the struck creature.** Join Railbreaker’s beam to its displaced targets, march Arc’s white contact along the chain, and let Meltdown light the undersides of feet against dark char. Preserve Whiteout’s ability to leave a hostile fan legible. `overdrive/gallery-overdrive-{breacher,arc,pyro,cryo}-crew-checkpoint-03.png`.
5. **Finish one marine animation and portrait pass together.** Plant the rear foot, compress the shoulder on a heavy shot and let the hips settle after the gun. Use a different silhouette-defining piece of kit for each selection portrait and carry the title’s visor and shoulder finish into those close views. The same work would improve the first choice and the most frequently seen actor. `motion/marine-contact-1.png`, `screen-extra/title.png`, `review-sheets/screens-05.png`, `screens-06.png`.
