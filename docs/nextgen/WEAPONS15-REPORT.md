## Candidate jobs

- **Pistol · Precision Finish** draws in 90 ms, fires a 44.55-damage opening shot after 650 ms of rest, and has infinite reserve for finishing an exposed target while another weapon reloads.
- **Rifle · Sustained Tracking** rewards keeping hits on one moving target with up to 22% extra damage, and loses 44% while armour remains.
- **Shotgun · Close Quarters** throws an eight-pellet fan up to about 205 units, staggers ordinary Crawlers and adds 36% damage during a recovery or stagger.
- **Plasma · Armour Break** puts a slow bolt through three bodies, dealing 2.35× damage to armour and venting after seven shots and topping up from its cell reserve.
- **Rocket · Group Break** breaks up grouped enemies and brood with radial damage, arms after travelling 170 units and can damage the marine after arming.

The pistol should feel like a short snap, the rifle a measured climb during a burst, the shotgun a shove, plasma a cyan plate strike, and the rocket a warm blast with bodies carried outward. Projectile, light, and particle work reuses the prepared presentation materials and pools.

## Evidence notes

Weapon damage counts actual enemy armour and health removed by player projectiles. Kills credit the terminal projectile. Grenades, status ticks, rig abilities and boon follow-ups remain outside direct weapon totals. Equipped time counts simulation time during combat including draw and reload. The all-damage column uses the run's full outgoing damage ledger. The matched Standard subset uses the original ten seeds per rig. Full confirmation adds the ten I14 supplemental seeds. Both difficulties use the same seed set.

The first tuning pass concentrated direct damage in plasma. In the first two complete Breacher Standard runs, plasma accounted for 53.0% of direct weapon damage, shotgun 3.7% and rocket 6.6%. The second tuning pass introduced a seven-shot thermal limit, made the shotgun recovery hit stronger at close range, and made the bot actively look for safe groups before spending a rocket. The two passes included no enemy health changes.

### Remaining weaknesses

- Sustained plasma trades its lead on plated targets for pauses during long fights. It clears the Warden's 120 armour in two nominal hits, but the Warden's total modelled health-to-zero time is longer with plasma than rifle.
- A rocket at 200 units is a poor use of the reserve against an isolated Brute or guardian. Safe group opportunities depend on open lanes and opponents staying together during projectile flight.
- The pistol's precision bonus is timed from its last shot. The HUD suggests the job and shows availability, but it has no dedicated precision-ready indicator.
- Background reloads settle their reserve transfer and build reload event. World feedback tied to the held-weapon reload lifecycle follows the held weapon.
