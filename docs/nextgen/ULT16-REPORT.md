# ULT16 ability aftermaths

The shared contact pool places the core at the native impact, placement or dash endpoint. At 60 Hz the local flash lasts up to three advancing frames. Its opacity follows flash intensity. The rig still draws its native hit footprint. A broken atlas mark develops under that footprint over 110 ms and fades over three seconds. The coloured point-light request lasts 320 ms on High. Low keeps the deck mark and two secondary accents. The same retained batches and V6 texture draw all of these marks.

## Critique and revisions

1. The final9 impact sheets showed a smooth orange centre in Meltdown. Railbreaker's beam merged with the white contact silhouettes. The first pass broke up the molten surface, replaced the fullscreen exposure kicks with local flashes, lowered the rail wake and gave each element a three-second contact mark. In the first Armory captures the hostile wedge remained readable, but Railbreaker's elevated core still outlasted its contact. Whiteout's rim dominated the victim. Slam's plates looked like spokes at 480×270. Arc's decorative enemy-to-enemy crawl implied hits outside its chain receipts.
2. The second pass shortened the white lance, moved its lingering energy into the trench, widened the raised plates, attached restrained ice shards to animated bones and removed the decorative enemy chain. At +300 ms the native Armory rail frame shows two dark displaced bodies separated from the trench. The Cryo fracture network was still nearly as bright as the targets. At +500 ms Slam's dust had largely replaced its lifted sectors. The final adjustments lowered frost crack brightness, extended the plate buckle, lengthened the slag glow and centred each Pyro shell sweep on its first native hit. Arc endpoints gained their own fading scorch.
