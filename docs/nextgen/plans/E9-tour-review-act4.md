# E9 Act4 tour art review

Reviewed raw baseline `artifacts/nextgen/e9-twenty-tours/` (manifest source `cde0d98351031c981cb6c38c6399fac5cef44093`). Each numbered PNG listed below was opened at its original 1920×1080 resolution and again as an exact pixel-area 480×270 reduction under `artifacts/nextgen/e9-twenty-tours-review/review480/`, using the existing `review480` image helper. These are static presentation observations, not gameplay or performance conclusions.

The later `e9-overhead-standard` capture is **outside this baseline review**. In particular the new Swarm South-airway exchanger is not present in the five baseline frames and receives no acceptance from these notes.

## Swarm Junction — five baseline frames opened

- **01 Spawn:** the three-mouth plenum and violet cysts immediately establish a corrupted ventilation plant. Its top lip and deep dark front have actual volume; nearby large blue-grey bank roofs still read as broad cutaway blocks. Floor zones differ in tone, but most of the lower/right crop is uninterrupted deck. Thin branching strands descending from the grille look more like graphic wires than thick organic growth. At 480 the plenum survives, while the floor remains the dominant impression.
- **02 Fight circle:** the bank/plenum faces at both margins and the northern throat explain the junction plan. Long inset grates give a service-floor purpose and the muted floor diagram stays below the marine. The central 40% is nevertheless sparse; the landmark is split off at the edges. This is a readable fight floor, not a richly inhabited crop.
- **03 North airway:** strongest enclosure in this tour. Near/far side faces, cap widths, far hull framing and wall-backed machinery make a genuine service passage. Violet and cyan rectangular light are backed by visible fixture details. The centre still contains essentially one small grate plus a long threshold grate. At 480 the side walls retain depth; instrument/growth details mostly collapse to dark silhouettes.
- **04 South airway:** weakest baseline Swarm crop. Apart from the grate under the marine and the rooted wall seam at the right, the middle/top is empty deck. Cropped plenum tops at the bottom do not supply a strong function landmark inside this window. The later overhead exchanger is intended for this exact deficiency, but must be reviewed from its newer source separately.
- **05 Exit:** the plenum, violet growth, hull window framing and physical green exit source give a good destination view. Floor zones and linear grates keep the approach legible. Large empty left/bottom floor areas persist; long thin seam roots are visually weaker than the plenum's compact cyst clusters.

**Swarm assessment:** ventilation identity is clear near banks, with improved wall depth and readable actor space. Baseline 02 and especially 04 do not yet meet the “something of interest in every 40% window” goal. The blank South-airway deficiency is not marked resolved by this review.

## Shielding Gate — five baseline frames opened

- **01 Spawn:** dark blue deck, a substantial machinery bank and the rear hull give an enclosed technical room. The large plain roof block at upper centre has height/contact shadow but little function detail. The visible floor lights have actual housings. Most of the marine's crop remains empty blue floor; at 480 this reads as a generic technical arena rather than specifically shielding.
- **02 Fight circle:** the calm calibration inlay and opposed long light channels preserve excellent marine separation. The apparent long “bars” are flush light channels, not walls; their shape is clear, but neither they nor the quiet inlay supply a commanding shielding landmark. The machinery is almost entirely cropped into opposite corners. Wall/casing and deck share a cool blue family in the rendered image, weakening the intended hue separation.
- **03 Inspection gallery:** the layered equipment bank, framed panels and luminous terminal give the best sense of working shield equipment. Body faces, upper surfaces and a base shadow are distinct; light spills from a visible source. A comb-like dark streaked patch is visible in the blue floor field near the right-hand threshold rectangle. Its runtime cause is not established by this still; it should not be described as clean material detail.
- **04 Sterilization bypass:** quiet and readable, but weakly furnished in the actual close crop. Almost all function equipment is on the bottom/right margins and the centre has one short lit channel. At 480 it becomes another blue floor window with a bright dash. The bypass does not look like a separate sterilization treatment at this scale.
- **05 Exit:** strongest motivated light/source combination: wall devices, the exit plate and a substantial near-side bank create a convincing mechanical destination. There is another visible dark stepped/comb patch in the upper-left threshold floor field. The low foreground mass has volume, although its broad blank cap is still diagram-like.

**Shielding assessment:** equipment is manufactured and grounded, and actor readability is strong. Baseline 01/02/04 remain too dominated by the same blue floor. The central shielding function and the distinct sterilization sub-space are weak; rendered wall/deck hue separation is less convincing than the written palette. The visible floor streaks need review before visual acceptance.

## Containment Annulus — five baseline frames opened

- **01 Spawn:** pale clinical floor against the dark core foundation gives a strong value step. The sector inlay suggests inspection/calibration and is subdued beneath the marine, but the real core is largely beyond the right edge. Entry source panels are physically visible. The bright floor arcs and channels currently carry more of the crop than the room's unique machinery.
- **02 Fight circle:** the core edge and buttressed platform finally enter the frame and explain containment. The playable surface is calm and the marine stays distinct. The broad foundation is still visually heavy and mostly flat. Fine pale comb/sawtooth strokes are visible around the foundation perimeter, especially the right-side transition; these look broken rather than like designed mechanical seams.
- **03 North inspection ring:** strongest functional view: framed north machinery, a low coupler/console cluster, the sealed core and a glimpse of its passenger-status display give a recognisable containment service ring. Light is visibly motivated by wall/console surfaces. The short floor channel beside the marine appears as a solid teal lid rather than a readable recessed service opening. The perimeter trim also contains jagged repeated pale strokes. At 480 the console/core grouping survives, while the seam defects turn into dirty edge noise.
- **04 South service ring:** the near core, paired low service machines and the opposite bank create a convincing continuous inspection route. The cap/face/base separation is readable on the small machines and the core buttresses. Broad diagonal-grain platform fields dominate material detail. The central floor channel is again opaque-looking, and the interrupted pale perimeter strokes remain visible on both sides.
- **05 Exit:** strongest narrative connection: “PASSENGER ALIVE” and “NOT ARMED / MANUAL ONLY” are visible at native size on the left while the exit/source cluster frames the right. The two surfaces are distinct in value. Several floor channels change abruptly from lit sections into opaque teal sections across the foundation boundary; they do not consistently read as open service channels. At 480 the core identity remains, but the small text mostly does not.

**Annulus assessment:** a distinct place because the retained core and status hardware are present in most crops. It still has the L8 weakness of a visually oversized foundation. Pale sawtooth/comb perimeter marks and partly opaque channel appearances are visible breaks, and the fight crop relies too heavily on a floor circle with the landmark at its margin. This is stronger in function than the Shielding baseline, but not visually clean enough to call finished.

## Manual Control Chamber — five baseline frames opened

- **01 Spawn:** warm stone floor versus dark blue wall/cabinet faces gives a convincing two-tone material split. The suspended warning sign immediately names the room and its human consequence. Its beam, hangers and dark sign case have real depth, but the close crop contains very little working control equipment beyond its edges. The quiet keyway floor diagram is appropriate, not generic confetti.
- **02 Fight circle:** the same clear authorization language and the two opposed keyway diagrams make function legible even in the open floor. The marine remains unobscured beneath/beside the overhead structure. The large transverse black beam dominates composition; without the text this would still be mostly bare beige deck. At 480 the sign remains the main function cue rather than the small furnishing detail.
- **03 Authorization booth:** best actual control-room interior: a warm lit terminal against the rear hull, near-side equipment, enclosed side banks and shaped source light put the marine inside a specific station. Wall/cap/body values separate clearly. The original terminal is recognisable, though the large nearby warning sign is still visually louder.
- **04 Fatal-cost gallery:** the sign communicates the irreversible decision very effectively, and a real terminal/service block enters the lower foreground. The upper/left floor field is broad and sparse. The sign and rail have substantial construction; the smaller physical console is partly beyond the HUD crop. At 480 the space reads from its sign, with the controls relegated to the bottom edge.
- **05 Exit:** the right wall has grounded casings, a strong pale cap and visible lamps; exit direction is clear. Warm/cool surface contrast is good. Several small orange floor pools are rounded/disc-like rather than tightly shaped to their fixture, so the “no disc pools” light direction is not fully met. The overhead sign again occupies more attention than the working machinery.

**Manual assessment:** strongest two-tone separation in this five-room subset and a very clear narrative function. The warning sign solves the “what is this room?” question, but much of the inhabitation is still at frame margins. It remains more an excellent warning-sign composition over an open deck than a densely authored control chamber. No new obvious geometry break was seen in these five stills; the concern is distribution and light shape, not actor occlusion.

## Overload Floor — five baseline frames opened

- **01 Spawn:** immediate reactor identity. The large layered core, white-hot centre, warm housing and deep blue deck supply the strongest motivated light/complementary surface separation in this subset. The outer casing has a bright cap, a dark near face and meaningful depth. The fight floor remains quiet. Stepped dark streaking is visible inside the upper rectangular floor field and in the lower field behind the HUD.
- **02 Fight circle:** the retained core remains a strong landmark in the gameplay crop and its light reaches the marine's space. Expansion-joint inlays are function-specific and subordinate to actors. The same comb/stepped patches remain visible above and below the fight floor; they look like rendering/layer interference rather than intentional wear. At 480 the reactor still reads immediately and the marine remains findable.
- **03 Reaction-feed threshold:** north machinery panels, a real finned service bay and the core rim compose an actual power-plant route. Warm sources are visible and the metal faces/caps have a clear value split. The upper bank is broad and repetitive, but this window has enough function information. The stepped floor patch at lower left persists beside the core-lit area.
- **04 Exhaust gallery:** weakest Overload crop. The top of frame still contains a recognisable core/rim and connected service parts, but the marine's own patch is mostly dark blue floor plus one light channel. The foreground bank shows almost exclusively its broad cap because the working face is outside the crop. At 480 the core gives context, while the exhaust-specific function is not obvious. A faint streaked rectangular floor field appears toward the upper right.
- **05 Exit mark:** good spatial contrast between the hot core at left and the dark outer wall/devices at right. Warm cap faces and small source lamps retain the material hierarchy. The broad central floor is sparse but clearly belongs to the reactor space; the character remains separate from the core glow. Another faint comb-shaped patch is visible in the lower outlined floor field. This saved tour mark is not evidence of a gameplay exit from the final holdout.

**Overload assessment:** strongest overall room identity and light motivation of these five, because the core remains present through most close crops. The outer exhaust window still needs more function detail, and the stepped floor fields prevent a clean final-art judgment. This static tour does not establish heavy-combat readability or performance.

## Priority and review boundary

All **25 native frames and all 25 exact 480×270 reductions** were opened: `room-tour-<room>-01.png` through `-05.png` for the five rooms above. Native images were read directly from the raw tour directory; only the assigned reduction files and this review document were written.

1. Review the visible **comb/stepped floor fields** first: Shielding 03/05, Annulus 01–05 around the foundation, Overload 01–05 in outlined floor panels. Their cause is unconfirmed here; they are visible image defects, not accepted surface texture.
2. **Weakest function crops:** baseline Swarm 04; Shielding 01/02/04. Most of their close window is floor, while the defining equipment lies at its margins. These remain below the every-40%-window goal in this source.
3. **Annulus** has a real landmark and narrative but retains an oversized foundation, noisy perimeter marks and inconsistently opaque-looking service channels.
4. **Manual Control** has the clearest surface hue/value split and very effective signage. The actual controls still sit too often at the frame edge, and rounded orange light pools at the exit weaken the fixture-shaped light direction.
5. **Overload** is the strongest mood/function composition here; its exhaust crop is still sparse. Its core does more environmental storytelling than another repeated floor treatment could.

No marine body was visibly hidden by the reviewed architecture/signs in these calm stills. No idle orange dashed placement rectangle was seen. These observations do not certify native movement, collision, ability use or the newer overhead revision. In particular **Swarm's newer South-airway exchanger remains for the lead's separate `e9-overhead-standard` review**.
