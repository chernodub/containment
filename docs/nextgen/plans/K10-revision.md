# K10 revision — anatomy, curved shells and rig colour

The lead rejected the first delivery as a box-based blockout. Preserve its skeleton contract, runtime selector/material ownership, tool hooks and native verification pipeline. Rebuild the art rather than add surface detail to that blockout.

The body starts as elliptical cross-section rings following the retained bones: broad chest, narrow waist, rounded pelvis, tapering arms, muscular thighs and calves. Subdivision smooths the joined lofts. Armour patches are taken from these surfaces, shrunk/offset onto them, solidified and edge-finished. White ceramic follows the anatomy; graphite joint fabric and shaped boots/fingered gauntlets separate articulation. No cuboid torso, shoulder, shin or helmet masses.

Three closed curved helmet options will be judged at 140px before selection. An emissive crown/dorsal optic must be visible from behind. Rigs own the collar, both shoulders and forearms as well as the back. Aim for 30–40% of the visible upper view in amber/hazard black, ice blue/frost white, orange-red/charcoal or violet/graphite, plus strong element emission. Legs keep warm-white guards so the person remains legible on dark floors.

Weapons come from designed side outlines and circular mechanical sections, with a short/fat shotgun, open-core plasma and actual shoulder-tube launcher. Actual high-detail subdivided/bevelled meshes bake normal/AO/curvature onto game geometry. Target 15–25k active marine triangles. Six further actual render/look/fix rounds, numbered 5–10, each include 1000px beauty, 140px gameplay view, and native old/new comparisons. Portraits and full-figure transparent renders are outputs of the accepted model.

Disjoint work: root body/rig geometry and final art direction; helmet source/options agent; weapon source/export agent; bake helper agent. Root reviews every delivery. Browser jobs stay serial; no motion/world/UI/simulation edits and no tests/gates.
