# K7 — creature and character design

Scope: actor presentation only, desktop. No simulation, world, camera, UI or ability edits. Preserve retained Unvanquished notices. Local commits, no push/PR.

## Standard and route decision

1. Freeze baseline distribution, capture Brute/Spitter/marine in bright Awakening, dark Infested and warm Overload; benchmark Relay/Crew, Cryo/Pyro High, three native 20s trials each with host load.
2. Evaluate a verified CC0 Quaternius Ultimate Monsters candidate against rebuilt retained anatomy on Brute. Candidate source goes only into a new empty gitignored `tools/assets-src/k7-quaternius-monsters/`. Never execute source content. Preserve page/license/archive/output hashes. Candidate is review-only until selected.
3. Three actual PNG critique rounds (native, exact 480 and owner-buffer black silhouettes), then select/propagate. Design test: three connected masses, recognizable front, substantial contact limbs, role posture before detail. Marine preserves cyan-white head and physical height.
4. Queen: approve value masses in a flat study before detail, then head, load-bearing limbs, specific structured target and physically different open/closed shell. Nests share its shell/tissue language.

## Risks and acceptance

- Resident geometry must keep one anatomy draw/actor and shared ownership; no per-frame geometry/material allocation or mid-combat shader variants.
- Collision envelope, native windup timings, frozen identity, corpse/ragdoll anchors, reset/disposal and Low remain asserted.
- Keep failed raw runs; after successful final review remove successful intermediate PNGs except one contact sheet/round. Keep before/final galleries and receipts.
- Required: npm ci/build, verify plus independently completed stages, all desktop smoke/authored/playtest gates, before/after benchmarks. Missing prerequisites or shared-host misses are reported without changing gates.
