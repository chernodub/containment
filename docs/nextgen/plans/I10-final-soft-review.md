Cost audit's total GL calls include blitFramebuffer and its state calls include framebuffer/draw-buffer/pixel-store restoration, but it has no dedicated blit counter. Use ordinary unprofiled five-repeat runs for final FPS/scale acceptance and keep per-pass diagnostics separate. The summary's current timingEligible filter excludes CPU profile, cost audit and flush, but does not explicitly exclude gpu-passes alone; do not accidentally pool such a cohort into ordinary acceptance.
- CPU/heap profiles and GL/matrix/particle audit wrappers add deliberate overhead and allocations. Summary correctly excludes profile/auditCosts. Fresh/sequential bench:rooms and the native Start/Continue loading audit must separately retain the code-fetch/asset/preparation hold. The room audit's total gotoRoom includes code and kit waits; post-ready and native-fire compile checks remain separate.
- SoftParticles diagnostics are cumulative for the page. A positive copies count proves depth copying occurred sometime in that page; a Low run's prior High warm state may contribute historical copies. Use allocated:false at Low plus per-trial/frame deltas for cost attribution, and preserve GL0/context invariants. - The all-room heavy inventory is fixed-step draw/compile evidence, not FPS. Native campaigns' fixed-tick update+render CPU submission values are likewise separate from sustained RAF cadence.
