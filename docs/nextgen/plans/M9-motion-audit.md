# M9 baseline motion audit

Written before production animation changes. Source b03697b1f4c6722b7864d86cda7696f26ca1db7b. All 30 paged contacts were actually opened by the motion lead, plus individual marine run/stop/fire/reload/switch/dash/hit/cast, Crawler attack/hit/run/spawn, Spitter attack/hit, Brute attack and Carrier death strips.

Scale: 0 absent/broken, 3 rigid prototype, 5 competent prototype, 7 strong indie, 9 premium reference. No score is a gameplay or hardware claim. These are native-pixel production-rig presentation replays at the settled gameplay projection, with frozen simulation verified. Twelve frames at four 60Hz steps; attack/death use24. Four-phase contacts supplement complete strips. Full strips and clock/hash receipts live in artifacts/nextgen/m9-final/before-fixed/.

The five story families, every family elite, Brute Warden and Carrier Matron are covered. The Queen is legacy saves only, outside the current20-room story. Native contact attackers have no wind-up window; do not invent one. The audit judges body motion separately from external ability/status particles and world lighting. Fire strips initially isolate shoulders; an additional unchanged-source weapon pool pass is retained.

## What is actually wrong

The marine has usable lower/upper separation but feet cannot match native travel with the current clip calibration. Stop/start leaves a frozen running stance. Recoil, reload and cast poses barely change silhouette; taking a hit does nothing. Enemy source rigs provide distinct anatomy and gait, but body warnings and reactions underuse it. Brute/Spitter warning duration is correct while their held body pose is weak. Every family shares the same tiny non-directional flinch. Frozen is the strongest existing status response. Shock body animation is absent. Native corpse physics already has real killing direction; elemental deaths do not have distinct physical punctuation.
