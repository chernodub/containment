# E9 tour review — security, cargo and communications

Reviewed all five saved tour positions for Crew Checkpoint, Armory, Freight Hold, Breached Loading Bay, Relay Racks and Transmission Chamber: 30 native PNGs from `artifacts/nextgen/e9-twenty-tours/`, each also opened at exact 480×270. The corresponding per-filename reductions are in `artifacts/nextgen/e9-twenty-tours-review/review480/`. These are visual observations of that snapshot, with no performance conclusions. The planned Freight/Transmission overhead fixtures are not present in this reviewed evidence.

## Crew Checkpoint

The paired scanner arches, upward-facing **05**, scanner glyph, sensor posts and directional floor markings now make the main hall recognisable at both sizes. The roof is manufactured equipment rather than a grey plank. Walls have readable pale caps, dark near faces and contact shadows. The north bypass (03) adds a baggage conveyor and locker faces; its booth still exposes more roof than working surface. The south bypass (04) is the weakest crop: broad quiet floor dominates, while equipment low in the frame is hidden or cropped. Exit (05) has useful paired terminal screens and threshold hardware. Sources are visible, but the repeated long luminous runs often read as surface bars. The hall succeeds more convincingly than the inhabited bypass spaces.

## Armory

The horseshoe issue counter and radial carousel give Spawn (01) and Port magazine (03) an immediate armory silhouette, still readable at 480×270. Broad pale tops, dark counter faces and base shadows give the landmark substantial volume. The muted weapon/inspection floor graphic helps the Fight circle (02) without competing with the actor. Much surrounding blue deck remains plain, and several issue surfaces look lightly stocked. Starboard fitting bay (04) and Exit (05) are weaker: the carousel moves largely outside the crop and a generic conveyor/locker bank becomes the main equipment. Cyan and white sources remain visible, though the common linear fixture vocabulary carries more of the lighting than a specific fitting-bay task light. Strong landmark; less specific supporting work areas.

## Freight Hold

The warm ochre deck, **07** lane, tow/dispatch lines, grating and grouped low cargo cases establish a cargo palette. Wall caps and shaded faces read as physical construction. Dispatch court (03) and Exit (05) are strongest: cases, the labelled lane and a wall-backed terminal group coexist in the crop. Spawn (01) is weak because cargo is clipped high and the console is partly behind the HUD; amber floor dominates. Loading control booth (04) contains no obvious operating desk in its main visible area and reads as an empty L-shaped enclosure. Lamps at the container bank motivate local pools, but there is little handling structure or ship height in these crops. The planned overhead cargo fixture addresses this current weakness; it has not been reviewed here.

## Breached Loading Bay

The large peeled metallic hull scar gives this room one of the clearest identities in this set. Spawn (01), Port docking apron (03), Pressure lock (04) and Exit (05) retain useful portions of the damage landmark, repair lamps and surrounding structure. At native size the torn, riveted sheet has detail; at 480×270 it sometimes reads as folded gold metal more strongly than an open rupture, but remains distinctive. The olive/slate deck, quiet circular loading inlay and grates support the working bay. The pressure-lock side adds terminals, valves and a substantial threshold. Fight circle (02) is weakest: damage is mostly clipped at the top and the large pad becomes the scene. Mounted repair lamps are convincing visible sources. Supporting equipment is still thin away from the scar.

## Relay Racks

The dark teal palette, repeated U-shaped relay bays, cabinets and paired consoles establish a communications machine room in Spawn (01) and Fight circle (02). Rack mass and pale crowns survive reduction; much front-panel detail collapses into black at 480×270. The walls have volume at native size, but their dark faces lose separation at the smaller size. The quiet service floor remains clear for the marine, with little visible distinction between sub-spaces. North cross aisle (03) is the weakest crop: consoles sit at the extreme top and the rack is clipped below, leaving a large dark middle. Console bay (04) also hides much of its useful equipment against the lower HUD/crop. Screens motivate light well; long luminous runs feel less attached to an inhabited service layout.

## Transmission Chamber

Uplink chancel (03) is the only crop with a strong transmission-specific landmark: the dish, equipment bank and bright console faces communicate its purpose at both sizes. In Spawn (01), Fight circle (02), Service airlock (04) and Exit (05), the dish is outside the crop. Low solid barriers have real caps, faces and shadows, but their repeated vented attachments resemble generic service furniture. The quiet antenna/radiation floor graphic is appropriate and readable, yet cannot carry the room alone. Service airlock (04) is especially empty; equipment is confined to the margins and no working airlock controls dominate the scene. Exit (05) gains a physical hull and threshold, but remains sparse. Luminous bars have visible housings but little apparent support. The planned overhead fixtures are justified; their result remains unreviewed.

## Weakest current crops

- Transmission Chamber: 04 Service airlock, then 01 Spawn and 02 Fight circle; the landmark is absent and the floor dominates.
- Freight Hold: 01 Spawn and 04 Loading control booth; cropped cargo and missing visible controls weaken the named function.
- Relay Racks: 03 North cross aisle and 04 Console bay; useful equipment falls on the frame edges and dark detail disappears at 480×270.
- Crew Checkpoint: 04 South bypass; the scanner hall's specificity does not carry into this crop.
- Armory: 04 Starboard fitting bay and 05 Exit; generic equipment carries too much of the local scene.
- Breached Loading Bay: 02 Fight circle; distinctive damage is mostly outside the crop.
