# COMM13 visual review

Evidence is under `artifacts/nextgen/comm13-final/`. Native frames are 1920×1080. Reviews also use individual 480×270 reductions.

1. **Siege, Transmission Chamber and Overload Floor.** The teal outer ring and shorter inner sweep stay distinct from the orange projectile and warning marks. The relay and objective panel show the same remaining seconds, including the paused nine seconds after crossing outside.
   At 480×270 the sweep is clearest on Overload Floor. The short timer still fits beneath the marine. Cover naturally interrupts the floor ring.

2. **Priority and resistance, Armory.** The gold crown, violet shield and RESISTED flash are visible above the Warden during Cryo Nova; the hit and hostile swing remain visible.
   At 480×270 the crown survives the frost burst. The shield icon and partial violet contour are finer and easier to miss.

3. **Ranged, Crew Checkpoint.** The bottom-edge diamond and inward chevron pulse during wind-up and persist through launch until the live projectile enters view. The nearby breach cue stays gold.
   At 480×270 the inward bearing and light diamond still separate from the quiet arrows. The release cue can sit near the weapon panel.

4. **Stalker, Awakening Bay, Crew Checkpoint and Relay Racks.** The capsule follows the native wind-up through strike, and the violet organ stays visible against the darker Relay Racks floor.
   At 480×270 the pounce line survives all three floors. The recovery lift is subtler, especially beside the marine.

5. **Encounter beats, Crew Checkpoint, Residential Gallery and Armory.** The gold doorway arrows and objective lines identify crossfire and flank arrivals. Armory shows five canceled reinforcements and then the remaining escort bearings.
   At 480×270 the edge shapes are easier to pick out than the low-key in-world breach flare. The cleanup brackets remain visible by the bottom edge.

**Twenty-enemy frame, Communal Atrium.** The native frame and reduction show one crown and shield with three thin tethers. The crawler’s orange strike footprint and marine remain the strongest elements. The shield contour has little contrast at quarter scale.
