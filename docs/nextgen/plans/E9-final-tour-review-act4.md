# E9 final Act4 tour art review

Frozen source: `artifacts/nextgen/e9-final-captures/`, build `b7b3d7882d5b4a7892f2f684e55b243040e4981c`. Native PNGs were opened directly at 1920×1080, followed individually by exact pixel-area 480×270 reductions made with the existing `review480` helper under `artifacts/nextgen/e9-final-captures-review/review480/`. These observations concern the saved calm presentation, not movement, collision or hardware performance. The final gallery copies belong under `artifacts/nextgen/e9-final/native/` and `review480/`.

## Swarm Junction

- **01 Spawn:** the large three-mouth plenum, dark roots and violet nodes establish corrupted ventilation immediately. Its bright upper edges, dark front and contact shadow have depth. Brown service floor versus dark central deck now makes the named areas more distinct. Lower/right floor remains broad and quiet, with function concentrated near the upper edge. At 480 the plenum survives; long organic strands still resemble thin wires.
- **02 Fight circle:** opposing fan banks and the north throat explain the junction. Drainage grilles and the low-contrast angular inspection diagram keep the marine clear. The centre remains sparse, and the defining machines are cropped at opposite margins. At 480 this still reads predominantly as an open service arena, although it belongs to the surrounding ventilation system.
- **03 North airway:** the strongest enclosed passage: thick side-bank caps, visible near faces, rear hull framing and wall machinery establish volume. Violet/cyan light has a visible rectangular source. The floor is cleaner and subordinate to actors. The small centre grille and long threshold grate give purpose, although the large unbroken bank tops remain plain. Wall depth survives the reduction.
- **04 South airway:** the new suspended exchanger fixes the baseline crop's missing landmark. Three upward-facing round fan mouths, framing, roots and violet nodes are immediately readable at native and 480. Its substantial dark front face gives height and the marine is clear beside it. The upper half remains open deck, but the window now contains a strong ventilation object rather than only a floor dash. The still does not by itself explain the suspension height; it can resemble a raised machine until contextualised by the cutaway view.
- **05 Exit:** a complete plenum, green exit source, framed hull and darker central floor create a convincing destination. The green spill has a visible physical origin and the exit plate stays clear. Left/bottom floor remains open; roots are thinner and less substantial than the compact cyst clusters. No obvious comb-shaped floor defect was seen in these five Swarm stills.

**Assessment:** Swarm's worst baseline crop is materially improved and its ventilation function now persists through the south view. The fight-circle crop remains the weakest distribution of room detail. The brown/dark floor zoning is more apparent than in the baseline, while the bank roofs still occupy large plain areas.

## Shielding Gate

- **01 Spawn:** manufactured shield machinery at the upper edge has panels, a fan, rails and a visible source. The adjacent bank shows a pale cap and dark near face; however, a broad blank cap and blue floor dominate. The short glass-lit channel is physically framed. At 480 the equipment is marginal and the room's specific shielding function is weak.
- **02 Fight circle:** calm calibration marks and two long light-channel runs preserve excellent marine separation. Shield hardware is cropped into the upper-left/lower-right corners. The rendered floor and wall/casing remain in similar cool blue families despite value separation. Fine diagonal hatch/comb strokes remain along the upper floor-zone boundary, especially around the long horizontal seam; they are fainter at 480 but visible at native. The field interiors are calmer than the baseline.
- **03 Inspection gallery:** strongest working-room view, with large framed apparatus, a luminous terminal and a substantial rear hull. Cap, face and base shadow are clear and the light source is visible. Small floor grilles have two lit ends. Narrow diagonal bands remain near the lower horizontal zone edge and the right-hand outlined field; these do not read as convincing authored material seams. They reduce to dark edge noise at 480.
- **04 Sterilization bypass:** readable actor space and solid near-side machinery, but the central crop is again mostly the same blue deck with one small lit channel. Working faces sit on the right/bottom margins. Neither a distinct sterilization floor material nor an unmistakable sterilization fixture dominates this window. It remains one of the weakest function crops in the five-room review.
- **05 Exit:** bright source panels, physical exit pad and a thick near-side bank make a grounded destination. Foreground machinery has shaped top surfaces and dark faces. The upper-left stepped boundary still has a fine diagonal fringe at native size. At 480 the brightest source/exit grouping survives, but much of the left half remains empty blue floor. A rounded source hotspot remains within the larger rectangular light spill.

**Assessment:** actual equipment and wall depth are present, and broad floor-field interference is reduced. The fine diagonal boundary bands are not completely gone. Shielding 01/02/04 remain too dependent on marginal equipment and a common blue floor, with less convincing hue distinction than the other reviewed rooms. This is the weakest room in the first two final tours.

## Containment Annulus

- **01 Spawn:** pale inspection floor and dark core foundation supply a clear value change. The quiet segmented calibration circle belongs to containment, but the actual core is almost outside the right edge and this crop is mostly floor. Framed light channels are uniform along their length. Fine diagonal grain is conspicuous across the broader floor bands, especially at the upper/right and left zone edges. At 480 the diagram and foundation survive, with less distracting fine texture.
- **02 Fight circle:** the buttressed core enters the right side and establishes purpose. Actor space stays quiet and the ring diagram is legible without competing with the marine. The foundation is still oversized and visually heavy. The baseline pale sawtooth strokes around its perimeter are no longer visible; the dark perimeter is continuous. Floor channels no longer change into solid teal sections midway through.
- **03 North inspection ring:** low service machines, the coupler/console, upper hull framing and core edge make an actual inspection route. Separate top/face/base values give the furniture volume and source panels motivate the rectangular light. Dense diagonal floor grain now fills much of the service strip and is more conspicuous than the quiet central diagram; it is a visual texture concern rather than a cause established by these stills. The small lit channel is consistently framed. At 480 the core/furniture grouping remains clear.
- **04 South service ring:** the broad core, layered buttresses, paired low machines and near bank make the ring recognisable at both sizes. The formerly broken perimeter is clean. The floor field has continuous fine diagonal grain, which makes this native crop busier than necessary. The lit floor channel has no obvious opaque middle segment. The marine remains separate from the machines and their source light.
- **05 Exit:** retained passenger/manual-only status displays and the source-lit exit make the room's story clear at native resolution. The dark foundation, pale circulation floor and light casing caps have strong value separation. Former half-lit channel transitions are gone. Broad patterned floor strips remain, but the old pale comb perimeter does not. The text loses precision at 480 while the core/exit composition still communicates the route.

**Assessment:** the concrete perimeter/channel breaks from the baseline are materially improved. Annulus has a strong retained landmark and grounded service clusters. Its foundation remains large, 01/02 still devote much of the frame to a floor circle, and the broad diagonal grain is unnecessarily prominent at native size. The image does not establish whether every instance of that grain is deliberate material treatment or residual layering.

## Manual Control Chamber

- **01 Spawn:** warm stone-grey floor versus dark navy casing faces gives the clearest hue/value separation in this subset. The suspended sign's beam, hangers and frame are dimensional and its authorization/fatal-cost text immediately explains the room. Quiet key diagrams and framed floor lights support the function. The crop still contains little working equipment beyond its edges; at 480 the sign is the main room cue.
- **02 Fight circle:** two opposing keyway inlays are function-specific, low contrast and subordinate to the marine. The sign remains readable and the marine is clear beside its hanging frame. A very broad beige deck dominates behind the beam. This is effective narrative composition, but actual controls are largely outside the window. No obvious floor interference was seen.
- **03 Authorization booth:** strongest inhabited control-room crop. A real terminal and cabinet line the rear hull, a near-side service case has a substantial cap/face, and warm rectangular source light shapes the floor. The contrasting dark bank gives enclosure. At 480 the terminal grouping survives, although the warning sign remains louder than the controls.
- **04 Fatal-cost gallery:** the readable sign gives a clear consequence and the foreground console/retained apparatus adds function. The working controls remain partly at the bottom edge, with open floor across the upper/left. Floor zoning and key marks are clean in the reviewed still. Small orange pools around the console are soft and rounded rather than tightly shaped to its panel.
- **05 Exit:** thick bank walls, pale case caps, inset face details and visible lamps frame a convincing exit. Warm floor against cool dark structure stays clear at 480. The sign remains a strong landmark, but it again has greater emphasis than the actual control station. Several rounded orange floor pools persist around the sources and exit plate; the fixture-shaped-light direction is not fully achieved.

**Assessment:** strong material distinction and unambiguous narrative identity, with clean floor layers in these stills. It still relies heavily on a large sign above a quiet deck. Working machinery distribution and rounded light pools remain weaker than its palette and signage. No marine body is visibly blocked by the sign in the saved frames.

## Overload Floor

- **01 Spawn:** immediate reactor identity from the layered core, white-hot centre, warm rim and dark blue deck. Cap, deep face and base are clearly distinct. Quiet expansion-joint inlays connect the playable floor to the machine. The former stepped patches inside outlined floor fields are absent; the central field is clean. A jagged dark shadow fringe remains along the warm outer reactor rim at native size, which is distinct from the former rectangular floor-patch issue.
- **02 Fight circle:** strongest landmark and motivated light in this group. The marine remains clear on the dark side of the core glow, and the low-contrast joint lines support a power-plant floor without filling the fight area. Both native and 480 retain the reactor's depth. The broad outlined floor fields remain clean. The dark rim shadow fringe is still visible near the left reactor edge.
- **03 Reaction-feed threshold:** rear hull panels, an actual finned service bay, a vented cabinet and the core rim make a recognisable technical route. Lamps have visible sources and warm/cool faces separate well. The large back bank is repetitive but dimensional. The baseline comb patch at lower left is gone; this floor now reads continuously.
- **04 Exhaust gallery:** weakest Overload crop. The rim and service fittings at the top keep reactor context, but the marine's area is mostly dark deck and small light channels. The foreground bank exposes a broad cap while its working face falls outside the crop. At 480 the reactor context survives, but exhaust-specific machinery is weak. No former rectangular interference patch was seen in the upper-right field.
- **05 Exit mark:** the hot core at left and dark source-backed casings at right frame a convincing outer reactor route. Large open floor remains calm and the marine stands apart from the glow. The formerly streaked lower floor field is clean. Red source spill is subtle and rounded near the exit mark. This is a saved tour position, not evidence that the final holdout has a gameplay exit.

**Assessment:** strongest overall room identity and light motivation in this subset, with the broad floor-layer defects cleared. The exhaust crop remains sparse, and the jagged reactor-rim shadow edge is visible at native size. It is materially cleaner than the baseline without losing the core's storytelling role.

Only this review and the assigned reductions were written. No idle orange dashed ability-placement rectangle was seen.
