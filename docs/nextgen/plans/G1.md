# G1 technical plan

Own the final image in DepthRenderer and preserve its public API. The integration seams are graphics options, room mood, camera presentation and a single post owner; simulation is unchanged.

- `post/`: explicit half-float HDR pipeline, depth AO, thresholded bloom, final grade/lens/haze, bounded quarter-resolution distortion. Low allocates no post targets.
- `lighting/`: ten original environment moods, fixed dynamic LightPool, room-local reflection capture. SceneLighting retains authored fixture positions and owns stable shadow projection.
- `atmosphere/`: bounded instanced particles, ground mist and motivated shafts; environment-specific, quiet behind threats.
- `camera/`: damped follow, aim lead, trauma/kick/punch with reduced-motion support. Evaluate long-lens perspective beside orthographic, retain all desktop edge framing guarantees.
- `vfx/`: preserve the bounded existing effect batches; upgrade HDR identities, persistent contacts and explosion stages, expose reusable ability primitives.

Parallel ownership: only lead edits DepthRenderer and graphicsOptions. Post worker owns post/** and its tests; lighting worker owns lighting/**, SceneLighting, EnvironmentMaterials and its tests; VFX worker owns vfx/**, AttackEffects, models, afflictions and their tests. Camera and atmosphere workers follow when slots free. Every worker reads the full brief and tests; no other stream files are edited.

Risks: WebGL shader variant prewarming, AO over budget, too many point-light shader slots, authored dry floor expectations, old source-string tests, tone mapping twice, contact fog occluding warnings. Measure captures on both tiers; cheap path stays forward only. Revisit lighting, haze and effect exposure after three full gallery critique/fix rounds. Gate with vitest, asset preview node tests, room evidence, build, smoke, authored-room smoke and verify (document known missing Blender).
