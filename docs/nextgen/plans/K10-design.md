# K10 design — Crown-keel pressure armour

This revised design supersedes the rejected box-based delivery. The rebuild plan was written in `K10-revision.md` before the anatomical work. Keep the existing skeleton, clips, runtime ownership and sockets; replace the art.

The ownable mark is a closed, swept pressure helmet with a single broad luminous crown keel returning over the back. It reads from the player’s usual high rear camera, where a conventional front visor disappears. The second mark is the exposed elemental rig structure: ram pistons, collared dewars, splayed hot stacks or open induction bows, echoed by the collar, shoulder and gauntlet shapes.

Build a person first. Elliptical cross-section rings follow the retained bones with a chest, waist, pelvis, tapered forearms, thighs and calves. Subdivide the lofts. Duplicate anatomical face regions for curved ceramic plates, fit them at a 1.5–3cm stand-off, solidify and bevel with weighted normals. Flexible graphite remains visible at neck, waist, elbows and knees. Hands have individual grouped phalanges and knuckles; boots have toe/instep shells, sole, heel bumper and ankle bearings. Primary shapes must never return to cuboids.

Warm-white ceramic and steel sit over a dark graphite undersuit. White front **and rear** leg shells keep the complete person visible on dark floors. Large flat zones carry the game’s ink/banded shading. Seams, release locks, vents, shell rims, hoses, bearings and retention hardware describe how the suit is built. Wear stays restrained on curvature edges; no all-over dirt/noise.

Aim for roughly 30–40% of the visible gameplay surface to carry the rig family. Judge the silhouette and colour together at a 140px figure and in a HUD-free 480×270 native room reduction. Do not rely on the backpack alone.

Three closed curved helmet studies were required before selection: Manta brow, Split crescent and Crown keel. Crown keel wins because its broad dorsal return remains one coherent mark at the gameplay size; the finer/split alternatives fragment. The visor is recessed and closed, with temple/jaw hardware and high-source service cuts.

Weapons begin as drawn polygon side profiles: receiver, grip, magazine, stock, handguard and sights. Extrude and chamfer; use real cylindrical bores/muzzle devices and openings. The pistol is compact with a hooked slide; the rifle has a long vented handguard and hollow stock; the shotgun is short, broad and twin-bored; plasma has an open luminous chamber/fork; rocket is a circular shoulder tube with a curved cradle. Use contrasting dark/light zones and energy emission. Preserve the exact attachment coordinates.

Avoid recognisable armour from other games, ribbed face helmets, heroic green power armour, skull/fantasy motifs and a colour-swapped common backpack. This is Containment’s industrial pressure equipment, with an original readable crown/rig vocabulary.

Normal, AO and geometric curvature come from real higher-subdivision/bevelled sources. Components separate for normal projection; very thin round hardware preserves its smooth low normals instead of accepting crossed-wall cage artifacts. Paint/emission bake directly from the low material zones. The final shared atlas is 2048²; active marine variants target 15–25k triangles. Preserve all animation/skeleton values exactly and verify through actual motion strips, room captures and campaigns. No motion/world/UI/simulation edits or new tests.
