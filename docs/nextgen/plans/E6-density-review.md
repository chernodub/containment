# E6 density revision after lead review

The lead accepted the imported domestic and engulfed-machine forms as progress, but rejected the predominance of empty floor and isolated islands. The five requested layers were functional floor surfaces/debris, continuous perimeter kits, camera-safe overhead/shadows, composed obstacle footprints and broad flat infestation creep. The original four kit-standard rounds and independent primary review remain historical evidence; none is relabeled as approval of this revision.

The partial corrected application207 gallery was stopped on that instruction and preserved unchanged as `artifacts/nextgen/e6-pre-density/`, with a rename/interruption receipt. Its twenty calm frames and three initial kiting frames are partial evidence, not a completed final gallery. Its completed hardware/readiness/scan cohorts are preserved as `e6-pre-density-bench`, `e6-pre-density-rooms` and `e6-pre-density-scan`.

## Density round2 — rejected
