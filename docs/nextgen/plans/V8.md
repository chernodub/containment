# V8 VFX review ledger

Baseline application: `7ceefa5a52cc74a6b95712f29653d9bf523906be`.
Read-only final5 references were actually opened from the main checkout: full Pyro/Arc Annulus phase04,
Whiteout/Meltdown Awakening phase04, Slam Awakening phase03, and organic Cryo Communal Atrium mid.
Only PNG files are used for visual review. No desktop-control or computer-use tools are used.

The initial art critique confirms the R10 defects: isolated similarly sized yellow flame islands,
bounded enemy scan rectangles, two near-white marine echoes, a clean-radius storm dominated by circles,
orange blocked Cryo preview, and bright rectangular Slam fragments.

## Method

The unchanged canonical V2 matrix retains all sixteen seven-phase abilities plus four full kits in
bright Containment Annulus and dark Infested Workshop (280 native frames /40 sheets per build).
The frozen before distribution and final shipping distribution are separate directories. Every
required native action, phase, priority, hero/HUD/AABB and fatal combat-program guard remains.
Three smaller critique rounds use `v8-round.mjs` and retain one contact PNG with source hashes each;
intermediate successful PNGs are removed only after final review. Failed raw runs remain untouched.
Exact480 reductions are desktop art review, never timing evidence.

Native benchmarks run alone, using ordinary production timing, identical room/rig/tier/seconds/repeat
arguments before and after. Best-of-N uses one jointly passing trial for its frame/scale/CPU/load;
peak draws and every failed trial remain. Host load is recorded, not treated as CPU utilization.

## Implementation constraints

No simulation, geometry/hit rules, world, actor or camera changes. UI change is restricted to the
existing placement-preview call site. Native status ownership routes live burning enemies through
the ability flame batch and suppresses duplicate fallback sprites. The legacy status shader remains
outside the listed ownership unless explicitly authorized.

No new GPU owner, material variant, third-party asset or runtime dependency is introduced. Retained
atlas licenses/manifests remain unchanged. Low stays reduced; all effects use existing pooled draws.

## Critique round 1 — bright room

Actually opened the fourteen-cell exact480 contact, native Flashfire03, Slam03,
Whiteout06, Overclock06 and Meltdown06. Connected fire and the single quiet ghost are
improvements. Reject Flashfire's smooth peach petal sheet: needs unequal cavities,
rolling folds and a stronger char edge. Reject Whiteout's huge rounded star junctions:
the sine-product veins create decorative geometry instead of frost. Reject remaining
amber Slam bodies as wood-like. Placement's rotating check reads as an arrow.

Fixes: asymmetric fire cavities/folds with a preserved spine and unchanged count;
finer constant-width frost veins and short twigs; physical Slam fragments all cool
steel; fixed-size view-upright validity glyph. The single subdued Overclock ghost,
removed scan quads, native threat priority and unobscured live marine are retained.
Contact: `artifacts/nextgen/v8/v8-round1/contact.png`.

## Critique round 2 — dark room

Actually opened native Flashfire03, Meltdown04 and Whiteout06. Fire has a clearer
directed spine, cavities and folds; provisional acceptance for Flashfire. Reject
Whiteout's warped continuous vein lattice: replace it with finite irregular
crystalline branch deposits separated by exposed steel. Reject three long parallel
Meltdown horns: shorten and vary suit exhaust while keeping the readable helmet,
weapon and actual hot vent hardware. Slam's cool steel palette is retained.
Contact: `artifacts/nextgen/v8/v8-round2-native/contact.png`.

## Critique round 3 — bright room

Actually opened native Meltdown04 and Whiteout06 plus the fourteen-cell contact.
Short suit exhaust is accepted for helmet/weapon clearance, though Meltdown is
still restrained rather than a room-dominating ultimate. Finite frost deposits
avoid the previous grid, but their first size reads as repeated fern icons.
Final correction makes the deposits smaller and varies secondary twig count.
The steel review also found that blue metal variants30/31 accidentally entered
the shared Cryo color heuristic; exclude those variants from crystalline glint
while retaining actual Cryo material response. No counts or GPU owners added.
Contact: `artifacts/nextgen/v8/v8-round3-native/contact.png`.

The rejected third-round application had a GLSL scalar/color name collision;
the native canvas guard rejected GL1282 before any PNG. Its failure remains at
