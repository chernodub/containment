## Lead review revision

The lead rejected the first look as too timid. Bold is now the default: 3 px actor silhouettes, 1.85 px architecture silhouettes, narrower major geometric creases, three diffuse values with 12% soft transitions, cool shadows and geometric bevel rims. Family-color actor albedo has three broad tones and stronger low-pass filtering. Fine floor grain/scratch work is skipped in bold. Subtle preserves the earlier treatment; medium and bold share the same shader programs. `PostPipeline.setStyleStrength()` is an opt-in art-review seam through the existing debug renderer; it does not add settings UI or globals.

Four-way evidence uses a frozen production build, matching clocks and assets, plus exact 600×400 crops and four-frame camera/actor motion rows. Final AA follows the ink/grade composite, with an sRGB RGBA8 attachment preserving shadow precision; HUD remains afterward at output resolution. This replaces the earlier scene-only AA kernel. Low explicitly prepares its actual HDR scene target before release, preserving zero-combat-compile invariants.

Timing remains an acceptance gate, not a staged-image claim. Metal elapsed queries in this session produced non-additive pass readings even with rotating isolated stages and explicit flush boundaries. Preserve raw results and mark attribution unsupported; use deterministic pass/pixel/work counts and ordinary frame distributions, with host load beside every timing.

Final pipeline tuning retains High FXAA, bounds Low AA to five fixed samples and skips actor silhouette depth fetches that never affect ID edges. Main scene color uses packed positive RGB11/11/10 HDR, dropping unused alpha; AO remains RGBA16F. Native packing pairs retain readability. Adaptive .70 endpoint pairs show genuine softness, recorded as a safety-net quality trade. Completed timings still miss strict acceptance; the final serialized desktop/room/fight receipts and UI ownership blocker are recorded in the report.
