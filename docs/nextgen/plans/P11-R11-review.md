## Atlas accounting for the incident pass

There are now **14 authored source packs: four E9 + ten P11**, including `p11-incidents`. Each inspected manifest records one 512×512 tangent normal atlas and `normalAtlas.estimatedRGBA8MipBytes = 1,398,101`. Total distinct authored normal-atlas storage bound: **19,573,414 bytes (~18.667 MiB)**. This is a calculated RGBA8+mip bound, not measured GPU-process memory. Embedded GLB image and external rebuild PNG are one runtime image, not two. AO/curvature remain vertex data.

The incident pack contains six models: abandoned-winch-repair, failed-shield-leaves, strained-containment-diaphragm, consuming-workstation-mantle, brood-stronghold-wall and failed-relay-patch. It adds one atlas (~1.333 MiB) to source requirements for Service Shaft, Shielding Gate, Containment Annulus, Infested Workshop, Swarm Junction and Relay Racks. Final incident pack: 6,324 triangles and 814,856 GLB bytes; its rebuilt normal relief was inspected before the final room pass. Low omits designated secondary geometry but retains warm buffers/materials/textures; no atlas-memory reduction is claimed. Source caches may retain packs from earlier rooms.
