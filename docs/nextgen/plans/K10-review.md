# K10 anatomical revision — actual render review

The four rounds in the earlier `b020c00` delivery reviewed a block-based model that the lead rejected. Their acceptance language is withdrawn. This diary covers seven further actual render → look → fix rounds, numbered 5–11, on the same branch. The accepted skeleton/runtime pipeline was preserved while the art was rebuilt around anatomical lofts and curved surface-derived shells.

## Round 5 — anatomical lofts and first curved shells

Actually opened the 1000px Breacher/Cryo beauties and the complete four-rig round5 contact (beauties, exact 140px gameplay figures and native-pixel old/new calm crops). The person now has a waist, shaped thighs/calves, fingered hands and curved boot/armour volumes. Native legs remain visible and all four colour families are immediately different. Early shell fits broke through each other and left unintended shoulder openings; fixed by resolving all shrinkwrap targets before joining, closing top caps, separating overlapping shell regions and holding boundary fit. This is still a smooth body blockout: balloon-like common shoulders, weak chest engineering and open-looking toes do not meet the final bar. The first helmet images were rejected as blank eggs; visor cutter winding and buried crown lighting were corrected.

## Round 6 — helmet selection and clean surface fitting

Opened all three corrected helmet beauty/gameplay studies and 140px contact. Choose option 3 Crown keel: its broad luminous dorsal return remains a coherent mark at a 28px helmet (~140px whole marine). Option 1 is too fine from behind; option 2's split marks fragment. The low has a real visor recess; higher-detail sources carry fine service cuts. Opened round6 beauty and the four-rig contact/native comparisons. Surface boundaries are cleaner; low retopology brings active variants near 20–22.5k triangles. Still rejected as a final: primary pauldrons/collars/gauntlets share too much shape; Breacher hazard enamel is partly buried, and toes need closed caps/soles. Next fix changes the actual kit silhouettes: low asymmetric ram armour, narrow insulated Cryo cup/collar, broad charcoal Pyro deflector with milled vents, and open Arc shoulder/gauntlet induction frames. Apply stripes to the finished outer armour, not the undersuit source.

## Round 7 — real kit proportions and closed boots

Opened every native 1000px beauty and the round7 board with exact 140px gameplay figures and native old/new crops. The narrower insulated Cryo cups and collar, asymmetric dark Pyro vent shield, and open Arc induction frames now change the shoulders/forearms as well as the packs. Toes are closed with an actual sole/ankle joint. Native colour IDs are much stronger than the original marine. Still weak: breast/thigh/greave surfaces are blank, and the geometric hazard bands are visibly fragmented rather than painted. Gameplay back-facing legs expose too much dark suit. Those are causes to correct, not acceptable polish limitations.

## Round 8 — actual high-to-low projection review

Opened the baked 1000px Breacher beauty and all four baked variants on the round8 board, including exact 140px figures and matched native old/new calm crops. Actual normal/AO/curvature projection works, but projecting colour from the high geometry stains shell rims and fine optics; the close-up is worse than the unbaked form study. Reject that bake. Keep high-to-low relief/occlusion, bake flat paint from the actual game mesh, and save RGB8 delivery maps instead of oversized float PNGs. Replace floating hazard patches with a procedural diagonal painted zone. Add curved service gaskets, breast locks, rib vents, shaped leg hatches and knee/greave seams to make the armour engineered rather than featureless. The same native room confirms the lower legs need light rear shells as well as front guards.

## Round 9 — readable machined surfaces and painted hazard zone

Opened native 1000px Breacher and Cryo beauties, then the complete four-rig board with 140px views and matched game crops. Hazard paint is now continuous, broad black/amber diagonal striping. Direct game-surface albedo eliminates the worst dark rim contamination. Curved breast service seams, release locks, ventilation, thigh hatches and knee/greave details make the surface purposeful without adding boxes. The full fifteen-view arsenal contact and all five native weapon beauties were opened: the compact pistol, hollow-stock rifle, short-fat twin bore shotgun, open plasma fork and round shoulder tube have visibly different massing. Remaining marine fixes: add rear leg ceramic so legs read from the actual camera, layer boot heel/ankle hardware, articulate the dark waist, and separate matching components during the normal bake to remove joint/plate projection contamination. The current helmet-jaw bake still looks soft in a large close-up.

## Round 10 — full-body value read, articulation and portrait inspection

Opened all four 1000px beauty views, Breacher front/back, and the four-rig board with exact 140px gameplay views and original/new native crops. Rear thigh/calf ceramic now keeps the whole person visible from the real camera; the layered boots have metal pivots/heel bumpers and the waist has flexible ribs. Active variants are 21,915–24,755 triangles after retopology. The single-command rebuild completed, including weapons, eight transparent UI PNGs and twist/crouch studies. The original skeleton node transforms, hierarchy, 122-joint order/inverse binds and all 17 clips/3,690 animation channels remain unchanged on direct inspection. Native captures in three rooms and heavy combat passed 28/28.

Opened the Breacher and Cryo bust/hero PNGs at their native1024px size. This exposed a remaining close-up defect missed by small views: pale coolant bands and thin coil fittings have dark, crossed-surface normal projection. Reject those normals. The character-scale cage is inappropriate for tiny tubular hardware, whose actual roundness already exists in the low geometry. Preserve their smooth low normals in the atlas; keep the exploded high-to-low bake on armour, helmet and relief-bearing hardware. This is a seventh corrective round, not a reason to label the current portraits final.


## Round 11 — corrected thin-hardware normals and frozen delivery review

Opened every final 1000px rig beauty, the complete 24-view front/side/back/top/beauty/gameplay board, exact 140px figures and matched native original/new crops. Pale tank bands/radiators and violet coil fittings now keep their intended smooth geometry and colour; the armour/helmet retain actual exploded high-to-low normal detail. Opened all eight final 1024px portrait/hero PNGs after the correction. Busts frame the helmet/shoulder/weapon; full figures keep both boots and the whole rig. Their original silhouette and flat colour zones now come from curved anatomical shells, not cuboids.

Opened the full 12-card HUD-free exact 480 sheet across Awakening Bay, Armory and Infested Workshop. Amber/striped ram mass, ice-blue twin vessels, orange splayed stacks/dark shield and violet open induction structures remain different without HUD identification. Opened the native Breacher calm capture and all eight artist twist/crouch images. No detached vertex, plate entering the helmet/pack or broken rigid weights appeared in those studies. Those posed Blender images do not certify native foot contact or incoming M10 clips.

The revised model is the frozen delivery candidate for native motion, campaigns and quiet benchmarks. Remaining close-up limits are clean/simple rear helmet and broad white fields, a few soft atlas-edge details on the jaw and thin panel seams, and stiffness inherited from the existing clips. Small hardware disappears at 480 as intended. Keep those limits explicit in the report. The earlier blockout acceptance claim is withdrawn; this judgement applies only to the anatomical Crown-keel revision.
