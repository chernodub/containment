# K10 actual render review

## Grey blockout — opened before detailing

Opened `turnarounds/blockout-breacher-gameplay.png` natively and `blockout-140-contact.png` with all four rigs reduced together. Breacher is low and asymmetric, Cryo reads as paired cylinders, Pyro has finned stacks, Arc an open yoke. Weak: helmet too square, cassette dominates, Cryo/Pyro both look like two cylinders, Arc's negative space is partly swallowed by the helmet. Fix: narrower split crown, deeper polygon chamfers, shorter Breacher cassette, more splay on Pyro exhausts and wider Arc opening. The blockout contact was a roughly 110px figure; the next contacts will explicitly crop to a 140px body height.

## Round 2 — all five views, production material ownership

Opened `round2-all-views.png`: all twenty front/side/back/top/gameplay renders. The back assemblies now have distinct physical structure from front and rear; the shoulder/forearm service insets help the suit look assembled rather than monolithic. Front views still feel utilitarian and mechanical; this is strongest from the intended camera, not as a cinematic face close-up. The first attempted production capture stopped on a title shader error: its per-mesh hook patched our shared material five times. Fix: independent material instances per skin, retaining shared atlas textures. Also repaired an empty boolean-generated material slot before baking, and reduced small-piece bevel subdivisions plus pressure-joint tessellation to get the live variant back inside the triangle goal. Indexed export now deduplicates equal corner attributes without altering skeleton data. These are the round-2 fixes, not hidden capture exemptions.

## Round 3 — native colour failure, then authored-zone integration

Opened the round-2 live Breacher PNG at native resolution and a nearest-pixel close crop. Skinning/hand attachment aligns with the existing pose, but almost the whole marine became the same grey. This is a real integration failure: GraphicSurface's photographic family remap replaced every sampled texture colour with one family tone. Fix: the K10-only material path retains original atlas values while continuing through the shared banded lighting and ink pipeline. No global art-strength change and no enemy material changes. Opened `round3-140-contact.png`; the reduced small bevels preserve the silhouette, now 12,012–13,516 triangles per live variant. Next inspect native run/fire/hit before accepting the finish and tune only the causes shown there.

## Round 4 — emission-mask correction and final deformation review

Opened the round-3 live calm Breacher/Arc PNGs and native run-90, fire-shotgun and hit strips. No exploded vertices, detached plate or hand/socket jumps showed through run/fire/hit; however the suit still washed out. Inspection of the actual generated maps found a second cause in the bake: Blender's default Principled emission colour is white even when its emission strength is zero. The emission-only bake had copied colour without strength, making ordinary plates/fabric emissive. Fix: multiply the emission colour by its authored strength before baking, so only the small optic/core windows emit. The atlas is rebuilt with that correction. The final close/native/reduced review, all-direction strips and three-room captures are recorded below after opening them; no claim of acceptance is made from an unviewed generated file.

Final actual-open acceptance: opened the twenty-view final turnaround board, the corrected emission atlas, the regenerated fifteen-view arsenal board and the eight artist twist/crouch pose previews. That correction changes only the preview, never the retained skeleton or production animation tracks. No plate cuts through the helmet/back cassette in the 70° torso study. These artist poses are not native foot-contact evidence.
