# V5 — graphic combustion and fractured constructs

## Direction and scope

Use one graphic grammar: a hot core, a saturated body and a dark/desaturated edge. Large asymmetrical masses carry identity at 480×270. Gases/light have interrupted silhouettes, never ink; solid constructs use chamfered/fractured geometry with restrained dark edges and three broad value steps. Seeded variation belongs to presentation only. Exact catalog/snapshot footprints remain authoritative.

Timing is anticipation → 34–50ms hot impact → shaped aftermath. Existing local priority masking and hostile order remain. Settled friendly surfaces yield after impact; attached suit features stay localized. No new draw family, render pass, texture family or mid-combat allocation. Low reduces detail, retaining primary silhouette and exact bounds.

## Language specification

- **Combustion:** three HDR bands in `GRAPHIC_FLAME_FRAGMENT`: dark red edge(.17,.007,.001), orange body(1.05,.105,.003), yellow-white core(2.8,1.9,.34). Tone mapping remains native. Two asymmetric silhouette bites, unequal lobes, a narrow pressure throat and a larger downstream mass replace uniform ribbons. All flame sources share this material; no gaseous ink. Explicit directed seeds stay stable during movement.
- **Solids:** shared clipped armor sections and five-sided fracture masses have three broad lighting values(.32/.65/1.12), with thin dark desaturated UV/barycentric edges. Ice crowns deform from stable seeds and interlock over one fractured waist. No rows of identical pyramids. Cover has two end assemblies, articulated projection and a solid cap.
- **Time/electricity:** preserve the established violet-white target chains. Attached suit/weapon edges are localized; echoes are one authored helmet/shoulder/weapon/split-stride contour each, mirrored toward the aim direction. World echoes use total ultimate age, so refresh never renews impact grace.
- **Beats:** native cast anticipation and exact catalog/snapshot bounds remain. `impactBeat` holds the pressure reference peak for50ms, then decays; Flashfire starts local actor yield at50ms and collapses by370ms. The aftermath is a dark scorch fan, sparse smoke or low fuel. Persistent flame cores remain physically hot, while broad coverage yields.
- **Cold budget:** an exact capsule replaces three overlapping strip outlines. Per-instance dominant-circle data fades only redundant interior pixels; Whiteout's outer boundary remains exact. Nova residue and settled trail detail also fade inside Whiteout. Frozen enemies get one shell. The storm consists of16/8 High/Low rounded vapor wisps and6/3 short outer currents, leaving its eye open.
- **Costs:** all shapes live in the existing eight instance batches, one shared boundary batch and one ribbon batch. No new render pass, texture, light pool or combat-time GPU object. Flat double-sided cards/boundaries submit once; solid transparency keeps its two-sided treatment. Low retains primary hit/cover silhouettes and reduces particles, currents, wisps, windings and electrical crackles. Existing inventory/disposal/priority-mask tests remain.

## Risks / acceptance

Normal-blended combustion must preserve hot cores without peach additive wash. Solid edges must not ink gases or add a pass. Broad impact coverage must decay immediately. Cryo overlap suppression must preserve exact standalone footprints and never change hit results. Afterimages must leave enemy silhouettes intact. Zero-increase draw/frame-p95 budget is strict; shared-host contention is reported, never used to claim acceptance.
