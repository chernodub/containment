# LOOK26 · Marine occlusion

The marine body and equipped rig draw into a cropped depth texture using the live skeleton. The nearest suit surface wins at each pixel. A single HUD quad compares that surface with the opaque scene depth and identity. Visible armor keeps its scene shading. Hidden armor gets a 55% dark fill and an approximately 1–2 native-pixel inside edge in the equipped rig color. The authored cyan optics come from the emissive atlas. The foot locator draws above the reveal. The weapon remains in the ordinary scene pass.

High and the Low preset borrow classified scene depth. Low with contours disabled uses the forward framebuffer’s depth test. The mask scales with the projected marine, in 64-pixel allocation increments. Both material programs go through room preparation. No gameplay systems changed.

The hostile warning union already had depth testing enabled. The shader now gives only its printed strokes foreground depth within 1.5 m of the marine. Fill and distant strokes keep their floor depth. This uses the existing warning draw.

## Draws and preparation

The Crew Checkpoint wall stun cost one additional draw on each preset. High went from 108 to 109 and Low from 88 to 89. Armory wall stun went from 106 to 107 on High. The original HUD already submitted the two visible skinned parts. The new path submits those skins into the patch and adds one composite quad. The measured combat program count was zero on both tiers. Explicit tier switches triggered preparation (264 programs before, 266 after across the two switches). The Low sample was taken after readiness while the simulation was held at wall stun.
