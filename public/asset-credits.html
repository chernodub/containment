Adaptation

Changes include conversion from IQE/MD3 to glTF, texture resizing and compression, animation sampling and trimming, repaired winding, tangent space and unweighted vertices, weapon sockets, game scale and material tinting. The retained alien models/textures and marine skeleton/animation data in assets/models/ are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0, with the source attributions above preserved.
