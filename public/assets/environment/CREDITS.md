Environment PBR materials by Poly Haven, CC0.

- https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate
- https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02
- https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete

Diffuse and OpenGL normal maps at 1024px; ARM maps at 512px. JPEG recompressed for delivery. ARM channels: ambient occlusion, roughness, metalness.

The `g2_deck_albedo.png`, `g2_deck_normal.png` and `g2_deck_arm.png` maps are original deterministic procedural artwork for Containment, under the repository [MIT license](../../../LICENSE). Reproduce with `node scripts/nextgen/g2-surfaces.mjs`; no external source images are used. The Poly Haven attribution above applies to the retained downloaded maps, not these three files.
