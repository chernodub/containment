# Character asset scope after I11

Shipping K11 creature bodies, elite/boss skins and textures; K12 marine body, four rig modules and textures; and K10 weapon meshes/textures are original repository-MIT artwork. No Unvanquished body, armour, creature or weapon mesh/texture remains in those shipping GLBs.

Unvanquished character skeletons, joint hierarchy/rest transforms, inverse bind data, attachment sockets and retained legacy animation clips remain. Source-derived animation data in `public/assets/models/motion/` also remain separately attributed. These data keep the Creative Commons terms and source contributors in `public/asset-credits.html`; MIT does not relicense them. Tyrant/legacy Queen attribution and legacy Queen overlay remain applicable.

The offline actor packer uses glTF Transform and meshoptimizer under their MIT licenses; see `glTF-Transform-LICENSE.txt` and `meshoptimizer-LICENSE.txt`. The runtime decoder bundled in Three.js uses meshoptimizer; existing Three and meshoptimizer notices are retained.
